## Meaning

- Mutually opposed or inconsistent.
- Useful in writing when discussing conflicting ideas or data.

---

## Word Family

- contradict(v)反駁、牴觸
- contradiction(n)矛盾、不一致

---

## Synonyms/Related phrase

- contradictory results / findings: 相互矛盾的（研究）結果

---

## Collocations

- A+be contradictory to+B: A與B矛盾

---

## Examples

- These two studies yielded contradictory results.
- The new rule is contradictory to our company's core values.
- I received contradictory instructions from two managers, so I don't know what to do.
