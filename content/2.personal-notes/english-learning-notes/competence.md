## Meaning

- The ability to do something well.

---

## Word Family

- compete(v)競爭、對抗
- competently(adv)稱職地
- competent(adj)有能力的、稱職的

* incompetent(adj)無能的、不稱職的
* competitive(adj)有競爭力的、好勝的
* incompetence(n)無能、不稱職（反義詞）
* competition(n)競爭、比賽
* competitor(n)競爭對手

- Competence-Competency(n)

|         | Competence                      | Competency                   |
| ------- | ------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| **詞性**  | 不可數名詞為主                         | 可數名詞                         |
| **焦點**  | 「狀態」或「整體能力」                     | 「具體的能力項目」                    |
| **抽象度** | 較抽象、理論性                         | 較實務、具體                       |
| **語域**  | 學術、理論、心理學                       | 商業、HR、評估                     |
| **例句**  | _linguistic competence_ (語言學概念) | _core competencies_ (核心能力指標) |

- 字根 petere（尋求、力求，源自拉丁 petere）— competence = com(一起) + petere = 相互競爭/力求 → 能力、勝任度
  - compete(v) 競爭 — com- + pete
  - competition(n) 競爭
  - competitor(n) 競爭對手
  - petition(n) 請願書 — 源自「要求」
  - appetite(n) 食慾 — ap(向) + petite

## Collocations

- competence+in+領域: 在某特定領域/學科上的能力

- someone be+competent+with+工具: 某人擅長使用某工具

- competence+of+someone: 某人/團體所擁有的能力

- competence+for+job/task: 有勝任某工作/職位/任務的能力

- Demonstrate / Show competence: 展現能力

- Achieve / Acquire / Gain competence: 獲得/培養能力

- Lack competence: 缺乏能力

- Core competence: 核心能力 / 核心競爭力

- Professional / Technical competence: 專業 / 技術能力

---

## Examples

- He demonstrated high competence in project management.
- All of our team members are competent with Excel.
- The success of the company depends on the competence of its employees
- The interviewer asked him to prove his competence for the job.
