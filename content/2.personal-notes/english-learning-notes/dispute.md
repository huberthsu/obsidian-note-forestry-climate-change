## Meaning

---

## Word Family

- disputable(adj)有爭議的
- indisputable(adj)無庸置疑的

---

## Synonyms/Related phrase

---

## Collocations

- dispute+over/regarding+something: 關於...的糾紛
- dispute+between a and b: a與b之間的糾紛
- dispute+with: 與...發生爭執
- in dispute還有爭議、尚未解決

---

## Examples

- If you have any disputes over the bill, please contact our customer service immediately.
