## Meaning

- To make more effort than required; to do more than what is expected or necessary.

---

## Word Breakdown

- **go** — 走、付出
- **the extra mile** — 額外的一英里（比喻超出預期的努力）

---

## Synonyms/Related phrase

- [[give it one's all]] — 全力以赴
- [[pull one's weight]] — 盡本分
- go above and beyond — 超出預期
- put in extra effort — 額外付出努力

---

## Usage & Collocations

- go the extra mile+for+someone: 為某人額外付出

- go the extra mile+to+V: 為了...額外付出努力

- go the extra mile+in+n(領域/工作): 在某個領域/範疇中加倍努力

- go the extra mile+on+something(某件事): 在某事上加倍努力

- willing to go the extra mile: 願意額外付出

- Always go the extra mile: 總是加倍努力

---

## Examples

- I am willing to go the extra mile for the team.
- Everyone goes the extra mile to ensure the event is successful.
