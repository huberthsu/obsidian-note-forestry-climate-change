## Meaning

- To change or vary irregularly, often in amount or level.

---

## Word Family

- fluctuation(s)(n)波動(通常是複數)
- fluctuating(adj)波動的、起伏的

---

## Synonyms/Related phrase

---

## Collocations

- fluctuate between A and B: 在 A 與 B 之間起伏／浮動

- fluctuate according to: 根據……而變動

- fluctuate wildly / dramatically: 劇烈波動、大起大落

- fluctuate slightly: 輕微波動、微幅震盪

---

## Examples

- Oil prices often fluctuate according to the international situation.
- Her mood fluctuates wildly throughout the day.
