## Meaning

- To suggest that someone should do something or that something is good
- To advise someone to try or consider something

---

## Word Family

- recommendation: 推薦、建議
- recommender: 寫推薦信的人

---

## Synonyms/Related phrase

- suggest
- advise

> [!note] 易混淆詞
> 與 [[commend]]（讚美）意思相近但用法不同，與 [[commence]]（開始）發音不相似但意思完全不同。詳見[[1.English words and phrases associations#commence--commend--recommend|詞彙關聯整理]]的三詞群組比較。

---

## Collocations

- recommend someone/something to someone: 向某人推薦某人/某事
- recommend + that 子句
- recommend+ving
- strongly recommend: 強烈推薦
- accept/follow a recommendation: 採納／聽從建議。

* make a recommendation: 提出建議

---

## Examples

- I recommend this restaurant to anyone who loves Italian food.
