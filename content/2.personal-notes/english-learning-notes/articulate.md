## Meaning

- (adj) Able to speak clearly and express ideas effectively; clearly expressed or pronounced
- (v) To express something clearly; to pronounce words distinctly

---

## Word Family

- articulation(n)發音、表達、關節
- articulately(adv)清晰地、清楚地
- inarticulate(adj)表達不清的、發音不清的
- 字根 articulus(源自拉丁 — 意為「關節」、「分段」→ 引申為「分節清楚地說」、「清晰表達」)

---

## Synonyms/Related phrase

- fluent
- clear

---

## Collocations

- articulate one's thoughts / ideas: 清晰地表達某人的想法／觀點

- articulate a vision/problem/need: 明確地闡述願景/問題/需求

- articulate speaker: 表達清晰的講者

- articulate presentation / argument: 條理分明、邏輯清晰的簡報／論點

- articulate words: 清晰發音

- highly articulate: 表達能力很強

---

## Examples

- During the project meeting, the manager asked every team member to articulate their thoughts.
- The interviewers were convinced by his articulate arguments because he could explain every concept easily.
