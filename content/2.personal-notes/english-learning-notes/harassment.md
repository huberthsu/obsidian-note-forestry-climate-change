## Meaning

- any repeated, unwelcome behavior that threatens, alarms, demeans, or causes emotional or physical distress to another person
- 持續、不受歡迎的行為，會對他人造成威脅、驚嚇、貶低，或引發情緒/身體傷害

---

## Word Family

- harassed(adj)感到疲憊不堪的、受困擾的
- harass(v)騷擾、糾纏
- harassing(adj)令人困擾的

---

## Synonyms/Related phrase

- bullying
- intimidation
- abuse

---

## Usage/Collocations

- 領域+harassment
  - sexual/online/workplace+harassment: 性/網路/職場騷擾
- suffer/experience+harassment: 遭受騷擾
- report harassment: 舉報騷擾
- prevent / stop / combat harassment: 預防/阻止/打擊騷擾

---

## Examples

- If you are experiencing workplace harassment, you should report it to HR immediately.
- The company has a zero-tolerance policy for any form of cyber harassment.
