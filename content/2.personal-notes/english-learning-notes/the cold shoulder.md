## Meaning

- to intentionally ignore, dismiss, or act unfriendly toward someone.

---

## Word Family

---

## Synonyms/Related phrase

- Freeze someone out: 排擠某人、把某人排除在群體以外
- Cut someone dead: 徹底當作空氣，裝作完全不認識

> [!note] 同主題群組比較
> 三個短語都涉及「故意冷淡或排斥他人」，但程度和使用情境不同。**the cold shoulder** 是基本的故意冷淡（一對一人際互動）；**freeze someone out** 強調群體性的排擠（團體、職場環境）；**cut someone dead** 是最極端的完全無視（程度最強）。詳見 [[1.English words and phrases associations#「冷淡對待/排斥」群組|「冷淡對待/排斥」群組]]

---

## Usage/Collocations

- give someone the cold shoulder: 故意冷落某人
- get the cold shoulder from someone: 受到某人的冷落

---

## Examples

- I’ve been getting the cold shoulder from my boyfriend since we had an argument last night.
- If you don't like what he did, then just tell him. Don't give him the cold shoulder.
