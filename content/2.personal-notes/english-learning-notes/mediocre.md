## Meaning

- only average or ordinary quality, neither very good nor very bad, and often slightly disappointing

---

## Word Family

- mediocrity(n)平庸之才、平庸的狀態、普通人
- 字根 med-(middle，源自拉丁 medius) + -ocr-(mountain，源自拉丁 ocris)
  — mediocre = 山腰既不上不下 → 象徵平庸、不突出、不優秀亦不劣質
  - medium(adj/n)中等的、適中的；媒介、媒體
  - median(adj/n)中位的、中間的；中位數、中線
  - immediate(adj)立即的、直接的 — im(不) + mediate
  - mediator(n)調解人 — med- + -ator

---

## Synonyms/Related phrase

- average
- so-so
- Meh

---

## Collocations

- mediocre performance/results/food/meal/service

---

## Examples

- If we don't introduce new technology, our company will see mediocre results very soon.
- The movie received mediocre reviews from critics.
