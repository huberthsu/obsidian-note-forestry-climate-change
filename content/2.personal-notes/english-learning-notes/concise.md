## Meaning

- giving the information needed without unnecessary words; brief and clear.

---

## Word Family

- concisely(adv) 簡潔地、簡明地
- conciseness(n) 簡潔、簡明
- 字根 cis（切割，源自拉丁 caedere「切割」）（Etymonline）— concise 源自拉丁 concisus「切斷的、簡短的」，是 concidere「切斷、切碎」的過去分詞；concidere = com-（此處可能為加強語氣字首）+ caedere「切割」
  - 🤖 **claudian:** _字根 cis 是「切割」，加強語氣的 com- 讓它變成「切得乾淨俐落」，像把多餘的文字都切掉，所以 concise 就是「簡潔清晰」的意思。_
  - incisive(adj) 敏銳的、尖銳的 — 源自拉丁 incīdere「切入」（in-「向內」+ caedere「切割」）的過去分詞詞幹 incis- + -ive；原為字面「切割的」，1850 年左右才有「思路敏銳、表達犀利」的比喻義（Etymonline）

## Usage/Collocations

- concise+something: 簡潔的...

- keep+something+concise: 保持簡潔

- be concise and to the point: 簡潔且切中要點

- clear and concise: 清楚簡潔

- in a concise manner: 以簡潔的方式

- concise summary / explanation / writing / language / answer

---

## Examples

- Before the meeting, we need to prepare a concise report for upper management.
- Please keep your email concise because the manager doesn't have time to read.
- During the presentation, please be concise and to the point when answering questions.
- Please explain the new process in a concise manner.
