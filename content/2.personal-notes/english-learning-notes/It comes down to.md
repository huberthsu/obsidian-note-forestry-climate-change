## Meaning

- The most important factor or the final deciding factor

---

## Word Family

- (短語無傳統字根變化)
- 相關詞彙: boil down to(歸根結柢), amount to(相等於), result in(導致)

## Collocations

- **Ultimately/In the end**, it comes down to+**noun phrase/verb-ing**
- It **all** comes down to

## Examples

- I thimk ultimately, it comes down to personal preferences.
- Success in language learning comes down to consistency.
