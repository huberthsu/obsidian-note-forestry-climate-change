## Meaning

- extremely complicated and difficult to follow or understand; (of a line, road, etc.) twisting and winding; (of reasoning or an argument) roundabout and hard to follow.

---

## Word Family

- convolution(n) 迴旋、複雜狀況
- 字根 volv（捲、轉，源自拉丁 volvere「捲、轉」，PIE 字根 \*wel-「轉、捲」）（Etymonline）— convoluted 是 convolute 的過去分詞/形容詞形式，convolute 源自拉丁 convolutus，= con-「一起」+ volvere「捲」，字面是「捲在一起」，引申為「複雜難懂的邏輯」
  - 🤖 **claudian:** _字根 volv 是「捲」，con + volv 是「捲在一起」，像捲成一團難以展開的東西，所以 convoluted 就是「邏輯纏繞、難以理解」。_
  - revolve(v)轉、旋轉 — re(再次) + volve「捲」
  - evolve(v)進化、發展 — ex(向外) + volve「捲」= 逐漸展開

## Usage/Collocations

- convoluted + 名詞：直接形容複雜的事物

- a convoluted way of + V-ing：用迂迴複雜的方式做某事

- become/get convoluted：變得複雜難懂

- convoluted plot / reasoning / explanation / argument / system / process
