## Meaning

- Likely to do or experience something

---

## Word Family

- proneness(n)傾向、易發性

---

## Synonyms/Related phrase

- likely
- inclined
- susceptible

---

## Collocations

- be prone to V-ing/n

- be prone to injury: 容易受傷

- be prone to mistakes / errors: 容易出錯

- accident-prone：容易發生意外的

- error-prone：容易出錯的

---

## Examples

- He is prone to catching colds in the winter.
- ChatGPT is an error-prone LLM model, so I need to double-check its content.
