## Meaning

- Used when describing someone who has excessive pride in themselves and behaves in a way that shows disrespect to others.
- Negative in tone, emphasizing unpleasant self-confidence.

---

## Word Family

- arrogantly(adv)傲慢地
- arrogance(n)傲慢、自大
- 字根 rogare（要求、主張）— arrogant = ar(ad-的變體，向) + rog(要求) + ant(...的人/性質) = 總是要求受人注目的
  - interrogate(v)審問、質詢 — inter(之間) + rogare
  - prerogative(n)特權 — prae(在先) + rogativa
