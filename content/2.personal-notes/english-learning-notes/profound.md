## Meaning

- Very deep or intense; showing great knowledge, insight, or understanding; of great importance or seriousness

---

## Word Family

- profoundly(adv)深刻地、深深地
- profundity(n)深刻、深遠、深意
- 字根 fundus（底部，源自拉丁 profundus「深的、無底的、廣大的」）（Etymonline）— profound 約 1300 年出現，意思是「有智識深度的、學識淵博的」，源自古法語 profont/profund（12 世紀）與直接源自拉丁 profundus「深的、無底的、廣大的」，也指「晦澀的；深奧的；過度的」，= pro「向前、向外」（PIE 字根 \*per-「向前」）+ fundus「底部」（見 fund n.）；拉丁文的字面義與比喻義並存，但英文已有 deep，因此主要用比喻義；「深切感受的、強烈的」義約 1400 年出現
  - 🤖 **claudian:** _字根 fundus 是「底部」，pro + fundus 是「向底部延伸」，一直往底部探去就是「深」，所以 profound 是「深刻的、深奧的」。_

## Collocations

- profound impact/effect/influence on+someone: 對...產生重大深遠的影響

- profound change in + 領域/對象/事物: 某個領域/對象/事物內部或本身發生了重大改變

- bring profound change to+someone: 為某對象帶來重大的改變

- profound implications for+領域/議題: 對...有重大且深遠的含意/連帶影響

- profound knowledge/truth/insight: 深刻的知識

- profound respect/gratitude/sadness/grief/regret: 深深的敬意/感謝/悲傷/悲痛/遺憾

---

## Examples

- The widespread use of smartphones has had a profound impact on modern society.
- In the past few years, there has been a profound change in the way we work.
- This new AI technology will have profound implications for the future job market.
- Rising global temperatures **have profound implications for** global agriculture.
