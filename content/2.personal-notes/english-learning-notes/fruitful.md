## Meaning

- Used when describing an effort or outcome that brings good results.

---

## Word Family

- fruitfully(adv)富有成效地
- fruitless(adj)徒勞的、無效果的
- frution(n)實現、成果

---

## Synonyms/Related phrase

- successful

---

## Collocations

- come to fruition實現；開花結果
- someone+bring+something+to+frution: 某人使某事實現

---

## Examples

- What a fruitful meeting we had!
