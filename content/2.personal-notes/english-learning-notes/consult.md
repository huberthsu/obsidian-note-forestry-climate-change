## Meaning

- Used when seeking advice or expertise

---

## Word Family

- consultation(n)諮詢、商議
- consultant(n)顧問
- consultative(adj)顧問的、諮詢的

---

## Synonyms/Related phrase

- talk with

---

## Collocations

- hold a consultation: 舉行諮詢會

* provide consultation; 提供諮詢服務
* seek a consultation: 尋求諮詢
* consult+someone: 向某人請教
* consult+something: 查閱某物
* consult with+someone: 與某人平等討論
* consult+someone+about+something: 向某人諮詢某事

---

## Examples

- Before making a final decision, we need to consult with the marketing team.
