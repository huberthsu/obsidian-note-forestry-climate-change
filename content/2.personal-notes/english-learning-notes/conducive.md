## Meaning

- Making a certain situation or outcome likely or possible

---

## Word Family

- 字根 duc/duct（引導，源自拉丁 ducere）— conducive = con(一起) + duc(引導) + ive = 一起引導向（某結果）的
  - conduct(v)引導、進行 — con(一起) + duc
  - introduce(v)介紹 — intro(向內) + duc
  - reduce(v)減少 — re(向後) + duc
