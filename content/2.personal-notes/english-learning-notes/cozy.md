## Meaning

- Feeling warm, comfortable, and pleasantly snug, like being wrapped in a soft blanket on a cold day.

---

## Word Family

- cozily(adv)舒適地、溫馨地
- coziness(n)舒適、溫馨感
- cozy up(v)親密依偎；指刻意討好、巴結某人

---

## Synonyms/Related phrase

| 詞            | 強調重點             | 例子                                              |
| ------------ | ---------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| **cozy**     | 整體溫馨、舒適、愜意的環境/感受 | _"Let's get cozy by the fireplace"_ — 營造溫馨舒適的氛圍 |
| **[[snug]]** | 緊密貼身 + 溫暖舒適的物理感  | _"The gloves fit snugly"_ — 手套剛好貼身合身            |

---

## Usage/Collocations

- cozy up/get cozy +on/by/in+地方: 窩在某個溫暖舒適的地方
- cozy up to/get cozy with+someone: 討好、巴結某人

---

## Examples

- It is so cold outside, let's cozy up by the fireplace and have some hot chocolate.
- Everyone can see he is trying to cozy up to the new supervisor to get a promotion.
