## Meaning

- Used when overseeing people or activities.

---

## Word Family

- supervision(n)監督管理
- supervisor(n)主管、監督者、論文指導教授
- supervisory(adj)監督的、管理的

---

## Synonyms/Related phrase

- look after

---

## Collocations

- supervise+(n)
- under the supervision of+someone：在某人/組織機構的監督/指導之下
- close / constant supervision：嚴密的/持續的監督
- lack of supervision：缺乏監督

---

## Examples

- He completed the research under the supervision of Professor Huang.
- The professor constantly supervises her students throughout their research.
