[[English Puns and Tongue Twisters]]

## Meaning

- To go somewhere with a person or group, usually when you have not been invited or are not a central part of the group.

---

## Word Family

- Tag-along(n/adj): 跟屁蟲、不請自來的人

---

## Synonyms/Related phrase

- join us: 較正式的邀請加入
- go with someone: 跟某人一起去

---

## Collocations

- tag along with+someone: 跟著某人

- tag along behind+someone: 在某人背後跟著

- Mind if I tag along?介意我跟著去嗎

- Want to tag along?想要一起去嗎

- Let someone tag along讓某人跟著去

---

## Examples

- We're going to the night market. Do you wanna tag along?
- **A:** "I'm going to Starbucks downstairs to get a coffee."  **B:** "Mind if **I** tag along? I wanna stretch my legs."
