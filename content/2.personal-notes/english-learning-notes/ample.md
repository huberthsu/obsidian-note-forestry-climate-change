## Meaning

- enough or more than enough; large in amount; (of space) large and providing plenty of room.

---

## Word Family

- amply(adv) 充分地、寬敞地
- amplitude(n) 振幅、廣闊、充分
- 字根 ampl（寬敞、廣大，源自拉丁 amplus「寬敞、廣大、豐富」）（Etymonline）— ample 源自拉丁 amplus，字面「寬敞」引申為「充足、豐富」；amplitude 是「寬度、廣度」的學術用語，常用於物理（波的振幅）
  - 🤖 **claudian:** _字根 ampl 是「寬敞、廣大」，所以 ample 就是「充足寬敞」的意思，帶有充分、足夠的正面感。_

## Usage/Collocations

- ample + 名詞：充足的...
- ample time / space / opportunity / evidence / resources
- have ample + 名詞：有充足的...

---

## Examples

- No rush, we have ample time to prepare the slides.
- There is ample room for the new product to grow.
