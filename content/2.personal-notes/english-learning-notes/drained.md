## Meaning

- Completely exhausted of energy, emotions, or resources; emptied of strength or vitality.

---

## Word Family

- drain(v) 流失、耗盡
- draining(adj) 令人疲倦的、消耗的
- drainage(n) 排水、耗損

---

## Synonyms/Related phrase

- [[fatigued]] — 疲勞的、疲倦的
- exhausted — 精疲力竭的
- [[deplete]]d — 耗盡的、減少的
- worn out — 筋疲力盡的

## Usage/Collocations

- drained by+n: 被...耗盡

- drained from+n/V-ing: 因...而耗盡

- emotionally/mentally/physically drained:  情感/精神/身體上筋疲力盡

- feel drained:  感到被掏空

- drained of energy:  精力耗盡

- mentally / physically drained: 精神上 / 體力上消耗殆盡

---

## Examples

- He felt mentally and emotionally drained from dealing with that difficult project.
