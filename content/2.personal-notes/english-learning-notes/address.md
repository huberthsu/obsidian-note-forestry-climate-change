## Meaning

- To deal with a problem or issue

---

## Word Family

- addressed(adj)已被處理的、已被寫上地址的
- addresser(n)寄件人、演說者

---

## Synonyms/Related phrase

- tackle
- fix
- handle
- deal with

> [!note] 易混淆詞
> 與 tackle / fix / handle / deal with 的區別見[[1.English words and phrases associations#「處理/應對」群組|詞彙關聯整理]]

---

## Collocations

- address the n(issue / problem / concern)+of+n/V-ing: 處理...的議題/問題/擔憂
- address the problem that+句子
- be addressed by + 方法/政策/人

---

## Examples

- The company is trying to address the problem of employees scrolling through social media during working hours.
