## Meaning

- Used for where someone lives, often in legal or official contexts.

---

## Word Family

- residence(n)住宅
- resident
  - (n)居民
  - (adj)居住的、常駐的
- residential (adj.)住宅區的、適合居住的
- 字根 sid/sed（坐，源自拉丁 sedere）— reside = re(持續) + sid(坐) = 長時間坐落於某處→居住
  - preside(v)主持 — prae(在前) + sid = 坐在前面主持
  - subside(v)平息、下沉 — sub(向下) + sid = 坐下去

---

## Synonyms/Related phrase

- live

---

## Collocations

- reside in+地方: 居住於某處
- reside with+人: 與某人同住

---

## Examples

- He resides in a quiet neighborhood.
