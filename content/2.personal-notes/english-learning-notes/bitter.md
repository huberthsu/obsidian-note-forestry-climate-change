## Meaning

- To be bitter means to feel deep disappointment, resentment, and sadness about a situation or outcome; to hold onto negative feelings about something that happened.
- Often describes a longer-lasting emotional state that combines disappointment with ongoing resentment, not just a momentary reaction.

---

## Word Family

- bitter(adj)苦澀的、怨恨的、失望的
- bitterly(adv)苦澀地、怨恨地、痛苦地
- bitterness(n)苦澀、怨恨、痛苦感

## Collocations

- be bitter about+n/V-ing: 對...感到失望且怨恨

- bitterly opposed to n/V-ing: 強烈反對...

- show no bitterness: 沒有流露出一絲怨恨

- bitter disappointment: 深深的失望

- fight/stay to the bitter end: 撐到最後一刻、奮戰到底（不論多艱辛）

- bitterly resent: 深深怨恨

- grow/become bitter: 變得怨恨、心態變壞

- remain bitter: 依然感到怨恨、無法釋懷

---

## Examples

- He is still bitter about not getting the promotion.
- Local residents are bitterly opposed to building a new factory near their community.
- Despite years of unfair treatment, she showed no bitterness.
- Since he lost the game, he has remained bitter.
