## Meaning

- Someone who is ambitious and takes initiative to achieve their goals

---

## Word Family

- A go-getting attitude 積極進取的態度
- A go-getting employee / professional 積極能幹的員工 / 專業人士

## Collocations

- a go-getter attitude/mentality: 積極進取的態度/心態
- a go-getter employee/sales person: 積極能幹的員工/銷售員
- self-motivated go-getter: 自律的積極進取者
- hire/recruit go-getters: 雇用/招聘積極進取的人
