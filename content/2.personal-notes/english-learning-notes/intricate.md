## Meaning

- having many small parts, details, or relationships arranged in a complicated, delicate, or deeply connected way.

---

## Word Family

- intricately(adv)複雜地、精細地
- intricacy/intricacies(n)錯綜複雜的細節、複雜性
- 字根 tric（麻煩、糾纏，源自拉丁 tricae「麻煩、阻礙、把戲」，複數名詞，來源不明）（Etymonline）— intricate 源自拉丁 intricatus「糾纏的」，是 intricare「使糾纏、使困惑」的過去分詞；intricare = in-「在…之中」+ tricae
  - 🤖 **claudian:** _字根 tric 是「麻煩、糾纏」，in + tric 是「陷入糾纏之中」，像精細手工層層纏繞起來的東西，所以 intricate 就是「複雜精密、錯綜複雜」。_
  - extricate(v) 解救、使脫身 — ex-「向外」+ tricae，字面「從糾纏中脫出」（Etymonline 與 intricate 交叉參照）

## Usage/Collocations

- be intricately designed/woven：設計／編織得很精細

- A be intricately linked with B：A與B複雜地聯繫在一起

- the intricacies of something：某事物的複雜細節

- intricate details / process / network / design / mechanism

---

## Examples

- The watch is intricately designed.
- Our company's success is intricately linked with customer trust.
- As a project manager, she needs to figure out the intricacies of new regulations.
