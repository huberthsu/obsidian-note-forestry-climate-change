## Meaning

- Grudge (noun): a persistent feeling of resentment; a deep-seated anger held against someone for a past offense.
- Grudge (verb): to resent someone for having something, or to give something unwillingly.
- Often describes long-held anger from a specific past event.

---

## Word Family

- grudging(adj)不甘心的、勉強的
- grudgingly(adv)不甘心地、勉強地

---

## Synonyms/Related phrase

- [[resent]] 對某人/某事感到不滿（可短可長期）
- resentment 怨恨、不滿（名詞形式，可指任何長短期不滿）

## Collocations

- have/hold a grudge against someone: 對某人心懷怨恨

- grudge someone something: 不甘心讓某人擁有某物、嫉妒
  - grudge him his success

- let go of a grudge: 放下怨恨、不計前嫌

- bear a grudge: 懷恨、記恨。純粹表示對某人心懷怨恨

- harbor a grudge: 心中藏著怨恨。強調暗地藏著不讓人看出

- nursing a grudge: 養著怨恨、持續記恨。強調刻意維持這種情緒

- grudge match: 報復賽、怨恨之戰

- not grudge+n/V-ing: 不吝於、不計較

---

## Examples

- Since they had the fight last year, she has borne a grudge against him.
- Are you grudging him his vacations
- He always harbors a grudge instead of speaking up
- She didn't grudge spending all her money to make her parents happy.
