## Meaning

- Confidently putting forward one's own views or values; not shy or hesitant.
- Behaving in a confident and forceful manner; standing up for one's rights or opinions.
- Tending to state things in a forceful, confident way.

---

## Word Family

- assertion(n) 主張、宣稱、自信
- assertively(adv) 自信果斷地、敢言地
- assertiveness(n) 自信果斷、敢言、果斷性
- assert(v) 主張、堅持、聲稱
- 字根 sert（連接、排列，源自拉丁 serere）— assert = as(向) + sert = 把自己的主張擺出來
  - insert(v)插入 — in(向內) + sert
  - exert(v)施展、發揮 — ex(向外) + sert

## Synonyms/Related phrase

- confident — 有自信的（正面，對自己有信心）
- [[arrogant]] — 傲慢的、自大的（負面，過度自信）
- bold — 大膽的、勇敢的
- outspoken — 直言不諱的、坦率的
- self-assured — 自我肯定的

## Collocations

- assertive in+V-ing/情境/領域: 在什麼環境下敢言

- assertive about+n(觀點/立場/權利): 在什麼事情上敢言

- assertive with+對象/人: 對什麼對象上敢言（用 with 不用 to，是因為這是「一來一往的應對過程」而非單向特質，見 [[2.Collocation rules for verbs,adjectives followed by prepositions#規律八：形容詞 + to 或 + with 的判斷|規律八]]）

- an assertive tone: 果斷的語氣

- take an assertive stance: 採取堅定的立場

- sound/get/seem assertive: 聽起來/變得/看起來 很堅定

- more/less assertive: 更/更不 敢於表達/堅定

- assertive personality / person: 堅定有自信的人格／人

## Examples

- In meetings, you need to be assertive about your rights while remaining polite to your colleagues.
- When you negotiate with aggressive salesmen, you have to be assertive with them.
