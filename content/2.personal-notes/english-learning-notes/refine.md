## Meaning

- Used to improve something in detail or precision.

---

## Word Family

- refinement(n)精煉、精進
- refined(adj)精煉的、精製的
- 字根 fin（界線、終點，源自拉丁 finis）— refine = re(反覆) + fin(終點) = 反覆提煉到最純粹的終點狀態
  - finish(v)完成 — 同源
  - define(v)定義 — de(劃出) + fin = 劃出界線

---

## Synonyms/Related phrase

- make better

> [!note] 特殊
> 與[[modify]]的比較：refine 是改進細節、使其更精緻完善；modify 是做出改變（可大可小、不一定是改進）

---

## Collocations

- refine a skill/plan/strategy/process/method
- refine oil / sugar / gold: 提煉石油／精煉糖／煉金
- years of refinement多年的精進

---

## Examples

- The process was refined for effciency.
