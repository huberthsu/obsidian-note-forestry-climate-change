## Meaning

- Flawless; having no defects or errors; beyond criticism or reproach

---

## Word Family

- impeccably(adv)完美無瑕地
- impeccability(n)完美性、無瑕
- peccable(adj)易出錯的、有缺陷的(相反詞)
- 字根 peccatum(源自拉丁 peccare — 犯錯、犯罪)
  - im-(否定) + peccable = 不會犯錯→完美無瑕
  - impeccable 常用於形容「品行無可指責」或「品質無缺陷」

---

## Synonyms/Related phrase

- flawless
- faultless
- perfect

## Collocations

- impeccable credentials/reputation/record: 無可挑剔的資歷/名譽/紀錄
- impeccable manners/behavior: 完美的禮貌/舉止
- impeccable taste/style: 絕佳的品味/風格
- impeccably dressed: 穿著完美無瑕
- impeccable source: 可靠無誤的來源

---

## Examples

- Her impeccable manners and taste in dress impressed everyone.
