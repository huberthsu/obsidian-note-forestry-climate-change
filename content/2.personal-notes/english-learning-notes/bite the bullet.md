## Meaning

- To start dealing with an unpleasant or dangerous situation that you cannot avoid any longer; to make a difficult decision or take a difficult action that is necessary but unwelcome.

---

## Synonyms/Related phrase

- face the music: 勇敢承受後果
- take the plunge: 做出大膽決定
- grin and bear it: 忍耐、咬緊牙根
- swallow the pill: 接受不愉快的事實

---

## Usage/Collocations

- have to bite the bullet: 必須咬緊牙根
- finally bite the bullet: 終於鼓起勇氣
- bite the bullet and do something: 硬著頭皮做某事
- force someone to bite the bullet: 迫使某人做出困難決定
