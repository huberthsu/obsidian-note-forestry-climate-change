## Meaning

- A list of things a person wants to do or achieve in their lifetime before they "kick the bucket" (die).
- Often used for dreams, goals, or experiences considered important or meaningful.

---

## Word Family

- kick the bucket 去世、死亡（俚語，bucket list 的字面來源）

---

## Synonyms/Related phrase

- life goals
- wish list
- dreams

---

## Collocations

- make a bucket list: 列出人生清單
- add sth to one's bucket list: 把某事加入人生清單
- check sth off one's bucket list: 完成清單上的某項
- have sth on one's bucket list: 某項在人生清單上
- bucket list item: 清單上的項目
- A bucket-list experience: 一生必體驗一次的經歷

---

## Examples

- Although I felt exhausted after the long flight, I finally checked watching the Northern Lights off my bucket list.
