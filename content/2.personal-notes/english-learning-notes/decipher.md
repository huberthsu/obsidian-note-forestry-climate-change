## Meaning

- To interpret or make sense of something that is difficult to understand

---

## Word Family

- decipherable (形容詞): 可破譯的、可辨認的。

* indecipherable (形容詞): 無法破譯的、字跡模糊無法辨認的
* cipher(n)密碼、暗號

---

## Synonyms/Related phrase

- decode
- interpret

> [!note] 同主題群組比較
> 詳見 [[1.English words and phrases associations#「理解/解讀」群組|詞彙關聯整理]]的「理解/解讀」群組，與 decode 和 interpret 的詳細比較

---

## Collocations

- decipher+n(handwriting / a signature/message/signal)

- hardly / barely decipher：勉強可以辨認

- successfully decipher: 成功破譯

---

## Examples

- I can barely decipher the doctor's handwriting on the prescription.
