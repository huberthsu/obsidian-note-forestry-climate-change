## Meaning

- To make suffering, deficiency, or a problem less severe.

---

## Word Family

- alleviation(n)減輕、緩和
- 字根 lev（輕，源自拉丁 levis）— alleviate = al(ad-,加強) + lev(輕) + iate = 使變輕→減輕、緩解
  - elevate(v)提升 — e(向外/向上) + lev = 向上舉起變輕
  - levity(n)輕浮、輕率 — 同源，取「輕」的引申義
