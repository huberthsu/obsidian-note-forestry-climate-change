## Meaning

- To think about something carefully and for a long time; to consider or contemplate something seriously.
- To weigh something mentally; to deliberate on something.

---

## Word Family

- ponderation(n) 思考、琢磨
- pondering(adj) 思考的、若有所思的
- ponderously(adv) 沉重地、沉思地
- 字根 pond/pend（重量、懸掛，源自拉丁 pondus/pendere）— ponder 本義是秤量（想法的輕重）→仔細思考
  - pending(adj)待定的、懸而未決的 — 同源
  - suspend(v)暫停、懸掛 — sub(下方) + pend
  - compensate(v)補償 — com(一起) + pens(秤重)

## Collocations

- ponder+over+something: 反覆琢磨

- ponder+on+something: 對...深思

- ponder whether/if/how/why: 思考是否/怎樣/為什麼

- ponder one's options/choices: 琢磨某人的選擇

- deeply ponder: 深思

- sit and ponder: 坐著思考

---

## Examples

- She is pondering whether she should move overseas for work.
- The entire R\&D(Research and Development) team spent days pondering over the technical issue.
- With the contract expiring soon, the company is carefully pondering its options on future cooperation.
