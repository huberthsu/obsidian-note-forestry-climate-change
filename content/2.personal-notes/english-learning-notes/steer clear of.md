## Meaning

- To avoid someone or something
- steer 是「駕駛、操縱（方向盤/船舵）」，clear of 是「保持距離、不碰撞」。
  - 意象是: 開著一艘船，遠遠看到前方有暗礁或暴風雨，於是「轉動船舵，繞道而行，保持安全距離

---

## Word Family

---

## Synonyms/Related phrase

- Stay away from

---

## Collocations

- steer clear of+n(要避開的人事物)

- steer clear of+V-ing: 主動避免做某個行為、動作

- steer clear of trouble: 遠離麻煩

- steer clear of conflict: 避免衝突、遠離是非

- steer clear of oily food: 少吃油膩食物

---

## Examples

- You should steer clear of talking about politics in the office.
- Our company should steer clear of risky investments.
