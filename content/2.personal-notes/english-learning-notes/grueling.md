## Meaning

- Extremely exhausting and tiring; describing something that requires continuous physical or mental effort and drains one's stamina and energy.

---

## Word Family

- gruelingly(adv)極度累人地
- gruelingness(n)極度疲憊（罕用）

---

## Synonyms/Related phrase

- [[arduous]]
- [[strenuous]]

> [!note] 相關詞
> 與 [[arduous]]、[[strenuous]] 以及 demanding 都涉及「艱辛、困難」，但艱辛的來源和程度有區別。詳見[[1.English words and phrases associations#「艱辛/困難」群組|詞彙關聯整理]]的四詞群組比較。

---

## Collocations

- grueling for + 人: 對…來說很累人

- endure / survive a grueling + n: 撐過艱鉅的...

- grueling schedule/competition/ training / regime: 繁重艱難累人的日程/競爭/訓練/計劃

- grueling interrogation / negotiation: 漫長疲憊的審問/談判

- grueling hours / day: 極耗時間的時段/一天

- a grueling pace: 疲勞、折磨人的步伐/速度/步調

---

## Examples

- After a grueling 14-hour intensive negotiation, both sides finally reached an agreement.
- I can't keep up with the grueling pace of this tech company; I need a break.
