## Meaning

- To do one's share of work; to contribute your fair portion of effort to a team or project.

---

## Word Breakdown

- **pull** — 拉、貢獻努力
- **one's weight** — 某人的份量/責任（比喻應承擔的份額）

---

## Synonyms/Related phrase

- do one's share: 做出自己的份額
- carry one's weight: 承擔自己的責任
- pull one's own weight: 自給自足、獨立
- Dead weight: 累贅、拖油瓶、指團隊中完全不出力、只會增加負擔的人)
- Free ride: 搭便車、只享受成果卻不付出

---

## Usage & Collocations

- pull one's weight in + n(團隊/部門/公司/群體/專案): 在...中盡本分
- fail to pull one's weight: 未能盡本分
- refuse to pull one's weight: 拒絕出力、拒絕盡本分
- expect someone to pull their weight: 期望某人盡本分
- pull one's full weight: 使出全力、100% 盡到本分

* hardly pull one's weight: 幾乎沒有出力、在團隊中偷懶

---

## Examples

- If everyone pulls their weight in the team, we can definitely finish the project on time.
- I've just had enough! He always fails to pull his weight in the team and slacks off all day.
- The supervisor said that she expected everyone to pull their full weight in this critical project.
