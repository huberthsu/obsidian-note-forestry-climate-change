## Meaning

- A person who advocates for a theory, proposal, or course of action.

---

## Word Family

- proposal(n)提議、提案
- proposition(n)主張、見解
- propose(v)提議、建議、求婚

---

## Synonyms/Related phrase

- Opponent: 反對者

---

## Collocations

- a proponent of + 某個想法/政策/作法
- a strong/staunch proponent: 堅定的支持者

---

## Examples

- Our [[supervise|supervisor]] is a strong proponent of remote working.
