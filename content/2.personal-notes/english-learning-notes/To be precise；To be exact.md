## Meaning

- Used to correct or clarify a previous statement by providing more exact information or detail
- Phrase to introduce a more accurate or precise version of what someone just said

---

## Word Breakdown

- **to be** — 是、成為
- **precise** — 準確的、精確的
- **exact** — 確切的、準確的（synonyms to precise）

---

## Synonyms/Related phrase

- More accurately — 更準確地說
- More precisely — 更精確地說
- Strictly speaking — 嚴格來說
- In fact / Actually — 實際上、其實
- More specifically — 更具體地說

## Usage/Collocations

### To be precise

- To be precise, + 句子：在句首使用，引出修正或澄清
- 句尾用法：句子+ to be precise（用逗號或破折號隔開），對前面陳述進行修正補充
- 焦點：修正概念或陳述的準確性

### To be exact

- To be exact, + 句子：在句首使用，引出確切的細節或數字
- 句尾用法：句子+ to be exact（用逗號或破折號隔開），對前面陳述進行補充
- 焦點：提供準確的數字、事實或細節
- 特點：更常用於數字、日期、確切事實的補充

### 其他修正片語

- more + 形容詞(accurately/precisely/specifically) + 句子：強調程度遞增的修正
- Strictly speaking, + 句子：用於強調定義或邊界上的精確性
- In fact/Actually, + 句子：最日常的用法，揭示事實或糾正誤解

---

## Examples

- He is a relative of mine. My cousin, to be precise.
- The project will cost around fifty thousand dollars—to be exact, \$52,500.
