## Meaning

- to make or become less in size, importance, or intensity; to reduce or lower.

---

## Word Family

- diminishing(adj) 逐漸減少的、遞減的
- diminished(adj)被減弱的、被貶低的
- 字根 min（小、少，源自拉丁 minuere「使變小」，PIE 字根 \*mei- (2)「小」）（Etymonline）— diminish 由兩個已廢棄的英文動詞 diminue 與 minish 合併而成：diminue 源自古法語 diminuer，源自拉丁 diminuere「打碎成小塊」（deminuere「減少」的變體，= de-「完全」+ minuere「使變小」）；minish 源自古法語 menuisier，同樣源自拉丁 minuere；diminishing returns 是經濟學術語，指每次增加投入帶來的效益遞減
  - 🤖 **claudian:** _字根 min 是「小」，de + minuere 是「完全變小」，所以 diminish 就是「減少、降低」。_
  - minor(adj) 較小的、次要的 — 拉丁 minor「較小的」，源自 minus
  - minimize(v) 最小化、降低到最少

## Usage/Collocations

- diminish in+方面：在...方面減少

- diminish+something：直接減少某物

- diminish over time：隨時間減少

- diminishing returns：遞減效益

- diminish in+value / importance / quality / effectiveness

---

## Examples

- This house has diminished in value in the past few years.
- The new manager's strict rules diminished team morale.
- Her passion for the job has diminished over time.
- Despite diminished resources, our team still achieved our goals last month.
