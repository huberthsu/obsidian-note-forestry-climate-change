## Meaning

- Present participle of "embark": boarding a ship or vessel for a voyage.
- More broadly, beginning or starting on a journey, project, adventure, or endeavor, often suggesting something new or uncertain.
- Implies commitment to a course of action, especially one that feels significant or carries some risk.

---

## Word Family

- embark(v)登船、著手、開始
- embarked(adj/v)已登船的；著手的
- embarkation(n)登船；起航；出發
- 字根 em-(在...之中，源自拉丁 in) + bark/barque(小船、船隻，源自拉丁 barca)
  - embark = 上船→引申為開始冒險、著手某事
  - Related: barge(n)駁船；disembark(v)下船

---

## Synonyms/Related phrase

- begin: 開始（更通用）
- start: 開始（中性、最通俗）
- commence: 開始（較正式）
- set out: 出發、開始（更具冒險意涵）
- undertake: 著手、進行（較正式，強調責任）

---

## Collocations

- embark on: 著手...、開始...
  - embark on a journey: 開始旅程
  - embark on a new career: 開啟新職業
  - embark on a project: 著手一項計畫
- embark for: 前往
- about to embark: 即將開始

---

## Examples

- After graduating from college, she is embarking on a brand new journey in life.
