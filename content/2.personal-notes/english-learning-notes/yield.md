## Meaning

- Used when stopping resistance or agreeing initial opposition

---

## Word Family

- yielding(adj)柔軟、有彈性的、順從的
- unyielding(adj)堅固的、不屈不撓的(正面)

---

## Synonyms/Related phrase

- give in

---

## Collocations

- yield to+對象/壓力: 向...妥協
- yield on+立場/議題: 在...上讓步
- yield+人+物: 把某物讓給某人

---

## Examples

- We will not yield to the buyers on the price.
