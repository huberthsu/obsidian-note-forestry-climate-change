## Meaning

- To take offense means to feel hurt, insulted, or disrespected by someone's words or actions; to interpret something as a personal attack or disrespect.
- Usually a short-term emotional reaction to something just said or done, even if the other person didn't intend it as an insult.

---

## Word Family

- offense(n)冒犯、不敬、犯罪
- offended(adj)被冒犯的、被傷害的
- offending(adj)令人厭惡的、冒犯性的
- 字根 fend/fens（打、撞，源自拉丁 -fendere「打擊」，僅見於複合詞）（Etymonline）— offense 14 世紀晚期出現，意思是「傷害、損害、痛苦」，也指「違法、不當行為；對上帝的過犯、罪」，以及「使他人不悅、傷害他人感情的行為」，源自古法語 ofense「冒犯、侮辱、錯誤」（13 世紀）與直接源自拉丁 offensa「冒犯、傷害、侮辱、罪行」，字面是「撞上」，為 offendere 的陰性過去分詞名詞用法；offend 源自拉丁 offendere「打、推、撞上」，引申為「絆倒、犯錯、使不悅、觸犯、激怒」，= ob「在前面、對著」（同化為 of-）+ -fendere「打擊」（見 defend）；「攻擊」義約 1400 年出現
  - 🤖 **claudian:** _字根 fendere 是「打擊」，ob + fendere 是「迎面撞上」，撞到別人的感受、規則或底線，就是冒犯，所以 offense 是「冒犯、罪行」，take offense 是「感到被冒犯」。_

## Synonyms/Related phrase

- be offended by: 被...冒犯
- take it personally: 把它當作人身攻擊

---

## Usage/Collocations

- take offense at+something : 因某事而感到被冒犯

- easily take offense: 容易感到被冒犯、玻璃心

- don't take offense: 別見怪、別生氣

- take great offense: 非常生氣、深感被冒犯

- no offense: 無意冒犯。通常在講一句可能不太好聽的話之前或之後加上

---

## Examples

- You shouldn't take offense at what he said
- You should be careful when talking to him because he easily takes offense.
- Don't take offense, I'm just telling the truth.
- The boss took great offense at his rude email.
