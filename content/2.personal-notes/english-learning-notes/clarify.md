## Meaning

- Used when making something easier to understand or more precise.

---

## Word Family

- clarification(n)澄清、說明
- clarifying(adj)有澄清作用的、淨化的
- 字根 clar（清楚、明亮，源自拉丁 clarus）— clarify = clar + ify(使...) = 使清楚
  - clarity(n)清晰、明確 — 同源
  - declare(v)宣布 — de(徹底) + clar
