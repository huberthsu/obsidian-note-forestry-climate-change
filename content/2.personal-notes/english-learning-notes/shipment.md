## Meaning

- An act of sending goods by ship or other transport
- A quantity of goods sent or transported in one batch
- The goods or cargo being transported

---

## Word Family

- ship(v/n)運送、船
- shipment(n)出貨、運貨、貨運量
- shipments(n-pl)複數、多筆出貨
- shipped(adj/v-past)已出貨的
- shipping(n)運送、航運、運費
- shipper(n)託運人、出貨方

---

## Synonyms/Related phrase

- cargo: 貨物（船、飛機、車輛裝載的貨物本身）
- freight: 貨運、運費（各種運輸方式都適用，如 sea freight、air freight）
- delivery: 遞送、交貨（更通用，包括小包裹）
- shipping: 運送行為或運費；shipment 則是「一批貨/一次運送」
- shipment 最常用於商業物流、供應鏈語境

---

## Collocations

- send/dispatch a shipment: 發送貨物
- receive a shipment: 收貨
- track a shipment: 追蹤貨物
- shipment tracking number: 貨運追蹤號碼
- shipment date/status: 出貨日期/狀態
- incoming/outgoing shipment: 進貨/出貨
- bulk shipment: 大宗出貨
- delayed shipment: 延遲出貨
- free shipping: 免運費（不說 free shipment）

---

## Examples

- Due to customs issues, this shipment has been delayed for three days.
- You can check your shipment status online anytime using the provided tracking number.
