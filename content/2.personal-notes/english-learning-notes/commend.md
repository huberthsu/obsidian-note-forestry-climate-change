## Meaning

- To praise or express approval of someone or something
- To recommend something to someone

---

## Word Family

- commendable:
- commendation:

---

## Synonyms/Related phrase

- praise泛指所有稱讚，最通用
- compliment日常互動上的讚美

> [!note] 易混淆詞
> 與 [[commence]]（開始）、[[recommend]]（推薦）、command(命令)易混淆。詳見[[English words and phrases associations#commence--commend--recommend|詞彙關聯整理]]的群組比較。

---

## Collocations

- commend someone for something: 因為某事讚美某人
- highly/highly commend: 高度讚揚。

* publicly commend: 公開表揚。

---

## Examples

- The manager publicly commended her for her excellent performance.
