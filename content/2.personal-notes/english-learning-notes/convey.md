## Meaning

- To communicate or make known; to transmit or pass along information, ideas, or feelings; to transport or carry

---

## Word Family

- conveyance(n)傳達、運送、交通工具、契約書
- conveyor(n)傳送帶、運送者
- conveyed(adj)被傳達的
- 字根 via（路，源自拉丁 via「路、道路」，PIE 字根 \*wegh-「行走、移動、乘載」）（Etymonline）— convey 14 世紀初出現，意思是「陪同」，14 世紀晚期起指「攜帶、運送」，源自盎格魯法語 conveier、古法語 convoiier「護送、陪同」，來自俗拉丁 \*conviare「陪同上路」= 拉丁 com「一起」+ via「路」；「透過傳遞來溝通」義 14 世紀晚期出現
  - 🤖 **claudian:** _字根 via 是「路」，com + via 是「一起在路上走」，陪著走、把東西帶著走，引申為把訊息「帶到、傳達」，所以 convey 是「傳達、運送」。_

## Usage/Collocations

- convey+something+to+someone: 向某人傳達某事（例：convey a message to someone）

- convey a message: 傳達訊息

- convey information: 傳遞資訊

- convey an idea: 表達想法

- convey emotion/feeling: 傳達情感

- convey gratitude: 表達感謝

- convey sympathy: 表示同情

- convey the impression: 給人印象

---

## Examples

- Please convey my sincere gratitude to your manager for her help last week.
- The advertisement's purpose is to convey a clear message to young customers.
