## Meaning

- Used in writing to discuss negative impacts or consequences of policies and actions.

---

## Word Family

- detriment(n)傷害、損害
  - to the detriment of: 損害到……、對……不利
    - He pursued business success to the detriment of his family life.

---

## Synonyms/Related phrase

- adverse
- harmful

---

## Collocations

- be detrimental to: 對...有害
- have a detrimental impact/effect on: 對...產生有害的影響

---

## Examples

- He pursued business success, but it had a detrimental impact on his family life.
