## Meaning

- used to describe something that happens or exists in most cases, common in academic or professional contexts.

- 字根 domin（主宰、支配，源自拉丁 dominus「主人」）— predominate = prae(在先) + domin(主宰) + ate = 佔主導地位

## Examples
