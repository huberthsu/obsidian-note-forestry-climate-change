## Meaning

- Requiring or involving intense physical effort or energy; describing activities or exercises that are vigorous and tiring.
- 引申義：形容態度或言語上竭盡全力、強烈的（常搭配 objection、denial、effort），如 _strenuous objection_（強烈反對）、_strenuously deny_（極力否認）。

---

## Word Family

- strenuously(adv)費力地、劇烈地
- strenuousness(n)劇烈、費力

## Collocations

- strenuous for + 人: 對…來說很費力

- strenuous exercise / workout/activity: 劇烈運動/健身/活動

- strenuous effort: 付出很多力氣的努力

- make/raise+strenuous objections: 提出強烈反對

- strenuously object+to+n

- strenuously deny / oppose+n: 極力否認/反對

---

## Examples

- The doctor advised him to avoid strenuous exercise for three months after heart surgery.
- I strenuously object to the opposing counsel's statement!
