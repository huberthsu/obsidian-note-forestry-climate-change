## Meaning

- Dishonest or illegal behavior by those in power.

---

## Word Family

- corrupt(v/adj)使墮落、腐化、破壞；腐敗的、貪污的、毀損的
- corruptible(adj)易腐化的、可收買的
- incorruptible(adj)清廉的、不能收買的
- 字根 rupt（破裂，源自拉丁 _rumpere_）— corruption 源自拉丁文 _corrumpere_ = com-/cor-(強化前綴) + rup(破裂) + tion = 「破壞、毀損」；14 世紀初指「物質腐爛」，後引申為「道德敗壞、貪污」
  - disrupt(v)破壞 — dis(分開) + rupt
  - interrupt(v)中斷 — inter(在...之間) + rupt
  - erupt(v)爆發 — e(向外) + rupt
  - rupture(n/v)破裂、決裂 — 同源

## Collocations

- corruption in+領域/地方/組織/產業
- corruption within+機構/體制內部
- corruption of+某對象/價值
  - corruption of morals (道德的淪喪)
  * corruption of power (權力的腐化)
  * corruption of data (資料的毀損)

* corruption among+群體/職位: 某群體中的腐敗
* corruption involving+具體行為/對象: 涉及某貪污行為或對象的腐敗

- Fight / Combat corruption: 打擊腐敗

* Root out / Eradicate corruption: 連根拔除/根絕貪污
* Expose corruption: 揭發腐敗
* Tackle / Address corruption: 處理/解決腐敗問題
* Political / Official corruption: 政治/官方貪污
* Corruption scandal: 貪污醜聞
* Corruption charges: 貪污指控
