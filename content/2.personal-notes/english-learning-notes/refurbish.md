## Meaning

- To renovate, restore, or refresh something by cleaning, repairing, or updating it, often to return it to good condition.
- To make something look new or improved again, typically by making improvements or repairs.
- Can apply to physical items (buildings, furniture, equipment) or more abstract things (reputation, image).

---

## Word Family

- refurbished(adj)翻新的、整修後的
- refurbishment(n)翻新、整修、整頓
- furbish(v)拋光、修復、擦亮（較少用的原始形式）
- 字根 re-(再、重新，拉丁前綴) + furbish(拋光、修復，源自古法文 forbir)— furbish 本身來自日耳曼語 \*furbjan「使有好外觀」
  - Related: refurbish 在 1600s 時被創造，意思與 furbish 相同但強調「重新」的概念

---

## Synonyms/Related phrase

- renovate: 重新整修、改造（較常用於建築物）
- restore: 恢復、修復（強調恢復原狀或功能）
- renew: 更新、重整（較廣泛）
- refactor: 重新整理、改進（技術領域常用）
- update: 更新、升級（較非正式）
- spruce up: 整理、使煥然一新（較口語、輕微改善）

---

## Collocations

- refurbish a building: 翻新建築物
- refurbish electronics/equipment: 整修電子產品/設備
- refurbished products: 翻新產品、整修產品
- refurbish the office: 重新整頓辦公室
- refurbishment project: 翻新項目
- completely refurbished: 完全翻新的

---

## Examples

- They plan to refurbish the old kitchen before moving into the new apartment.
