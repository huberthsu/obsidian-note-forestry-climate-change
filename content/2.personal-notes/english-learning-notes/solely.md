## Meaning

- Not involving anyone or anything else
- Useful in writing to clarify specific points or responsibilities

---

## Word Family

- sole(adj)單一的、獨自的
- solitude(n)獨處、孤獨（通常指享受一個人的狀態）

---

## Synonyms/Related phrase

- only

---

## Collocations

- A+be+solely responsible for+B：A對B是唯一該負全責的人
- solely+because of/due to: 完全是因為
- based solely on: 完全根據
- solely on: 完全集中在/完全依賴
- solely for: 完全為了

---

## Examples

- The manager will be solely responsible for the failure of the project.
- I downloaded this app solely for fun.
