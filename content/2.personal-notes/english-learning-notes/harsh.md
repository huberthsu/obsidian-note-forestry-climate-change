## Meaning

- unpleasantly severe, cruel, rough, or more extreme than is necessary

---

## Word Family

- harshly(adv)嚴厲地、苛刻地

---

## Synonyms/Related phrase

- severe: 嚴重的、嚴厲的

* stern: 嚴厲冷漠的
* rough: 粗糙、粗暴的
* brutal: 殘暴的

---

## Usage/Collocations

- harsh+n
  - harsh reality: 殘酷的現實
  * harsh criticism: 嚴厲的批評
  * harsh words: 難聽的話 / 嚴厲的字眼
  * harsh punishment/penalty:嚴厲的懲罰
  * harsh conditions: 艱苦的條件
  * harsh climate/weather: 惡劣的氣候/天氣

---

## Examples

- Some employees are afraid of harsh criticism, so they don't dare to report any workplace harassment.
