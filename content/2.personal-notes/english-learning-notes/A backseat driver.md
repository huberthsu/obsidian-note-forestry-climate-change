## Meaning

- Someone who gives unwanted advice or tries to control a situation.

---

## Word Family

- Backseat driving 指手畫腳、什麼都要管的行為
  - I am tired of your **backseat driving** in this relationship.
- Backseat-drive 對……指手畫腳
  - Please don't **backseat-drive** my kitchen; I know how to cook a steak
