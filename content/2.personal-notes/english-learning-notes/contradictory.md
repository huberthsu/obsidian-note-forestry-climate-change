## Meaning

- Mutually opposed or inconsistent.
- Useful in writing when discussing conflicting ideas or data.

---

## Word Family

- contradict(v)反駁、牴觸
- contradiction(n)矛盾、不一致
- 字根 dic/dict（說，源自拉丁 dicere）— contradictory = contra(相反) + dict(說) + ory = 說相反話的
  - dictate(v)口述、規定 — 同源
  - predict(v)預測 — pre(事先) + dict
  - verdict(n)判決 — ver(真實) + dict = 說出真相

---

## Synonyms/Related phrase

- contradictory results / findings: 相互矛盾的（研究）結果

---

## Collocations

- A+be contradictory to+B: A與B矛盾（用 to 不用 against，是因為這是靜態的邏輯對應關係，沒有誰主動對抗誰，見 [[2.Collocation rules for verbs,adjectives followed by prepositions#against vs to：有沒有「主動對抗」的動作感|規律一 against vs to]]）

---

## Examples

- These two studies yielded contradictory results.
- The new rule is contradictory to our company's core values.
- I received contradictory instructions from two managers, so I don't know what to do.
