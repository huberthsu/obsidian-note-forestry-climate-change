## Meaning

- To arrange for financial protection against potential loss or damage by purchasing an insurance policy.
- Used in formal/financial contexts.

---

## Word Family

- insurance (n.)  保險
- insurer (n.) 保險公司、保險人
- uninsured (adj.) 未投保的
- 字根 sure（確定、安全）— insure = in(使...處於) + sure = 使處於安全狀態→投保
  - 與 [[assure]]（as+sure）、[[ensure]]（en+sure）同字根，差別只在前綴

---

## Synonyms/Related phrase

- protect (against risk)
- cover

> [!note] 易混淆詞
> 與 ensure / assure 的區別見[[1.English words and phrases associations#ensure--assure--insure|詞彙關聯整理]]

---

## Collocations

- insure against something: 針對某種風險/損失購買保險
- insure something for 金額: 為某物購買特定額度的保險
- insure + object + for 金額+against 風險: 為某物投保多少金額，以防範某種風險

---

## Examples

- To [[ensure]] our assets are protected, the company insured the building for 10 million dollars against fire.
