## Meaning

- Used when delaying somethimg to a later time.

---

## Word Family

- postponement(n)延期、延緩
- postponable(adj)可延期的、可推遲的

---

## Synonyms/Related phrase

- delay
- put off

---

## Collocations

- postpone+until+時間: 延期到某個時間
- postpone+to+時間: 延後安排某個時間點

> [!note] 細微用法差異
> postpone+until: 延期到這個時間為止，在那個時間之前，這件事都不會發生
> ex: The match was postponed until tomorrow.
> postpone+to: 延後到某個新的確切時間點
> ex: The meeting was postponed to 3 p.m.

- postpone+because of / due to+原因: 因為某原因而延期

---

## Examples

- The event was postponed until further notice.
