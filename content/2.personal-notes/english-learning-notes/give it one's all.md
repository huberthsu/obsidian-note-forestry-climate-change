## Meaning

- To put in maximum effort; to try as hard as you can; to dedicate all your effort and energy to something.

---

## Word Breakdown

- **give** — 給予、獻出
- **it** — 指代努力/能力
- **one's all** — 某人的全力

---

## Synonyms/Related phrase

- [[go the extra mile]] — 額外付出努力
- put in maximum effort — 付出最大努力
- try one's best — 盡力而為
- dedicate oneself to — 全身心投入

---

## Usage & Collocations

- give it one's all+in+n(活動/項目/比賽): 在...中全力以赴

- give it one's all+to+v(achieve/win): 為了(贏得/達成)...全力以赴

- give it one's all+for+n(目標/人):為了(目標/人)全力以赴

- determined to give it one's all: 決心全力以赴

- _one's: my, your, his, her, their, our_

---

## Examples

- The players gave it their all on the court to win the game.
