## Meaning

- Used when resources are completely used, often with energy or money.

---

## Word Family

- depletion(n)消耗、枯竭
- depleted(adj)枯竭的
- 字根 plere（填滿、充滿）— deplete = de(去除) + plere = 去除填充→耗盡
  - complete(v)完成 — com(一起) + plere
  - supplement(n)補充品 — sub(下方) + plere

---

## Synonyms/Related phrase

- use up, drain

---

## Collocations

- deplete+n
- depletion of+名詞
- severely/completely/gradually+deplete: 嚴重/完全/逐漸耗盡

---

## Examples

- The project depleted their budget.
