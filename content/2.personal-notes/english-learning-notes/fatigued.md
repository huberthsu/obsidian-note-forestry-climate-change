## Meaning

- Very tired, either physically or mentally; exhausted.

---

## Word Family

- fatigue(n) 疲勞、疲倦
- fatiguing(adj) 令人疲倦的、累人的
- fatiguingly(adv) 令人疲倦地
- 字根 fatig（使疲累，源自拉丁 fatigare，可能由 fatis「足夠」+ agere「驅使、做」組成，原意是「驅使到極限、精疲力盡」）— fatigued = fatigue + ed = 疲勞的
  - indefatigable(adj)不知疲倦的、不屈不撓的 — in(不) + de + fatig + able = 無法被累垮的

---

## Synonyms/Related phrase

- tired — 累的、疲倦的
- exhausted — 精疲力竭的
- weary — 疲憊的、厭倦的
- [[drained]] — 筋疲力盡的

## Collocations

- be fatigued by+something(疲憊來源): 被...弄得疲倦

- be fatigued from+something/V-ing(疲憊原因): 因...而疲倦

- chronically fatigued: 長期/慢性疲勞的（醫學上常用）

- mentally/physically fatigued: 精神/身體疲勞

- easily/constantly fatigued: 輕易地/持續地 感到疲倦

- feel/look fatigued: 感到/看起來 疲倦

---

## Examples

- The workers were fatigued from working under the burning sun for ten hours.
- I am already fatigued by the endless office politics.
