## Meaning

- To produce by assembling information from other sources
- Often used in academic writing and research discussions.

---

## Word Family

- compiler(n)(電腦)編譯器；編纂者
- compilation(n)合輯、彙編、精選輯
- 字根 pil（壓緊、堆疊，源自拉丁 pilare）— compile = com(一起) + pil + e = 堆疊在一起→編纂、彙整
  - pile(n)堆、疊 — 同源

## Collocations

- compile something from+來源: 從...彙整

* compile something into+成果: 把...彙編成...
* compile something for+對象/目的: 為了...彙編
* compile something with+工具/軟體

---

## Examples

- The research team compiled the report from different academic papers to provide policymakers with a fair and objective analysis.
