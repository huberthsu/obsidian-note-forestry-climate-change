## Meaning

- To communicate or make known; to transmit or pass along information, ideas, or feelings; to transport or carry

---

## Word Family

- conveyance(n)傳達、運送、交通工具、契約書
- conveyor(n)傳送帶、運送者
- conveyed(adj)被傳達的
- 字根 via(源自拉丁 via — 意為「路」)
  - con-(一起) + via(路) = 一起走的路→帶著傳達

---

## Synonyms/Related phrase

- express
- impart

> [!note] 群組比較
> 與 express、impart 同屬「表達/傳遞」群組比較，完整比較見 [[1.English words and phrases associations#「表達/傳遞」群組]]

---

## Collocations

- convey+something+to+someone+for+原因/對方做的事: 因為某原因而把某事傳達給某人

- convey a message: 傳達訊息

- convey information: 傳遞資訊

- convey an idea: 表達想法

- convey emotion/feeling: 傳達情感

- convey gratitude: 表達感謝

- convey sympathy: 表示同情

- convey the impression: 給人印象

---

## Examples

- Please convey my sincere gratitude to your manager for her help last week.
- The advertisement's purpose is to convey a clear message to young customers.
