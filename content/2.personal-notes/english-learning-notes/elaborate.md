## Meaning

- To add more details or information to what you have already said.
- To develop or work out an idea/plan in more detail.

---

## Word Family

- elaboration(n) 詳述、詳細說明
- elaborated(adj) 精心設計的、詳細的
- elaborately(adv) 詳細地、精巧地
- 字根 labor（工作、勞動，源自拉丁 labor）— elaborate = e(徹底) + labor + ate = 徹底加工→精心製作、詳述
  - laboratory(n)實驗室 — 同源
  - collaborate(v)合作 — com(一起) + labor

## Collocations

- elaborate on + 話題/想法:  詳述...

- elaborate details: 詳細的細節

- elaborate plan/design: 精心設計的計畫/設計

- further elaboration: 進一步的說明

- fully elaboration: 完整的說明

---

## Examples

- Can you further elaborate on the idea behind this design.
