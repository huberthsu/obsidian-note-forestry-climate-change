## Meaning

- To say or show that what someone is saying is true; to support or provide evidence for a claim.
- To provide support or help for someone or something.
- To make a copy of computer information as protection against loss.
- (American English) To make a vehicle move backwards.
- To move backwards; to retreat physically.
- When traffic backs up, it forms a long line of vehicles that cannot move.
- When plumbing backs up, it becomes blocked so water cannot flow out.

---

## Synonyms/Related phrase

- **Support meaning:** verify, confirm, substantiate, corroborate
- **Help meaning:** support, assist, aid, stand by
- **Computer meaning:** save, duplicate, archive
- **Movement meaning:** reverse, retreat, recede

---

## Usage/Collocations

- back up someone's claim/statement/accusation: 支持某人的主張/陳述/指控
- back up with evidence/data/proof: 用證據/數據/證明來支持
- back someone up: 支持某人
- back up your files: 備份你的檔案
- back the car up: 把車倒回去
- back up a little/bit: 往後退一點
