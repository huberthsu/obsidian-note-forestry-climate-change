## Meaning

- Resent means to feel angry, bitter, or upset about a person, action, or situation because you feel it is unfair or you have been hurt.

---

## Word Family

- resentment(n)怨恨、不滿
- resentful(adj)憤恨不平的、怨恨的
- resentfully(adv)憤恨地、怨恨地
- 字根 sent/senti（感覺，源自拉丁 sentire「感覺、思考」）（Etymonline）— resent 約 1600 年出現，意思是「感到痛苦或苦惱」（已廢用）；1620 年代起指「把（某事）當成傷害或冒犯、對…感到一定程度的憤怒或被激怒」，源自法語 ressentir「感到痛苦、後悔」，來自古法語 resentir「再次感覺、依序感覺」（13 世紀），= re-（這裡或許是加強語氣的前綴）+ sentir「感覺」，源自拉丁 sentire（見 sense n.）；英文中曾有「欣賞、感激」的正面義（1640 年代），已廢用
  - 🤖 **claudian:** _字根 sentire 是「感覺」，re + sentire 是「再感覺一次、反覆感受」，把受到的冒犯反覆在心裡感受，怒氣就留下來，所以 resent 是「怨恨、憤慨」。_

## Collocations

- resent+n/V-ing: 不滿某事或做某事

- resent someone for doing something: 對某人因做了某事而怨恨

- resentment+against/toward+someone: 對某人的怨恨

- resentment+at/over+something: 對某事的怨恨

- resentful+of+someone/something

- harbor/feel resentment: 心中懷有怨恨

- stir up resentment: 挑起怨恨

- lingering resentment: 揮之不去的怨恨

- mutual resentment: 互相的怨恨

- bitterly/deeply resent: 深深地怨恨

---

## Examples

- I resent being forced to work on weekends.
- She resented her parents for making decisions for her.
- He harbors resentment against his ex-boss.
- She is resentful of being left out of the project.
