## Meaning

- a quality, feeling, or ability that you are born with rather than one you learn

---

## Word Family

- innately(adv)天生地
- innateness(n)天生性、先天特質、內在本質
- 字根 in-(內、於...之中) + -nat-(born，源自拉丁 nasci "to be born")
  — innate = 於生時內在具備的 = 與生俱來的
  - natal(adj)出生的、與出生相關的
  - native(adj)本土的、原生的、土著的
  - natural(adj)自然的、天生的
  - prenatal(adj)產前的
  - neonatal(adj)新生兒的
  - naturalize(v)歸化、使自然化

---

## Synonyms/Related phrase

- inborn
- inherent
- natural

---

## Collocations

- innate+talent for+V-ing: 天生有做某事的才華
- innate+ability to+v: 天生有做某事的能力
- innate capacity to learn: 天生的學習能力
- innate sense of+特質
  - innate sense of justice / rhythm: 天生的正義感/節奏感
- innate tendency to + V: 天生有做某事的傾向

---

## Examples

- He has an innate talent for calming down the furious customer.
- Everyone has innate creativity, but not everyone will develop it.
