## Meaning

- responsible for your actions.

---

## Word Family

- accountability(n)問責性、負有解釋與承擔後果的責任
- 字根 put（思考、計算，源自拉丁 putare）— accountable = account(計算、帳目) + able = 可被計算/被要求交代的
  - dispute(v)爭執 — dis(分開) + put = 雙方計算不合
  - compute(v)計算 — com(一起) + put = 一起計算
  - deputy(n)副手、代理人 — de(從) + put = 被委派的人

---

## Collocations

- A+hold B accountable+for+something: A要求B為某事負責
- A+be accountable to+B+for+something: A在某件事上，必須向B負責/交代
- someone+be held accountable+for+something: 某人因某事而被追究責任
- hold oneself accountable+for+something: 為某人自己的某事負責

---

## Examples

- We must hold large corporations accountable for their detrimental effect on the environment.
- As a project manager, you are accountable to the CEO for the budget.
- If we cannot get the job done on time, the manager will be held accountable.
- You should hold yourself accountable for your paper.
