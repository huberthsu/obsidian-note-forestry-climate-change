## Meaning

- The state of being calm and in control of emotions; calmness and poise under pressure

---

## Word Family

- compose(v)組成、撰寫、平靜
- composed(adj)鎮靜的、冷靜的
- composer(n)作曲家
- composition(n)組成、作曲
- 字根 compositus(源自拉丁 componere — com(一起) + ponere(放置) = 組合、安排)
  - 引申義：身心的「組合」或「平衡」→ 冷靜穩定的狀態

---

- equanimity

## Collocations

- maintain/keep one's composure: 保持冷靜
- lose one's composure: 失去冷靜
- regain one's composure: 恢復鎮靜
- with composure: 沉著地
- remarkable/admirable/unshakeable composure: 非凡的/令人欽佩的/不動搖的冷靜

---

## Examples

- Despite the sharp questions from the interviewer, she still maintained her composure and answered confidently.
- To avoid losing his temper at the client, he went outside and took a deep breath to regain his composure.
