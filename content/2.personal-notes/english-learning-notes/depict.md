## Meaning

- To represent by a drawing, painting, or other art form.

---

## Word Family

- depiction(n)描繪、描述、肖像
- depicted(adj)被描繪的
- 字根 pict（畫、描繪，源自拉丁 pingere，過去分詞 pictus）— depict = de(徹底) + pict = 徹底畫出→描繪
  - picture(n)圖畫 — 同源
  - pigment(n)顏料 — 同源

---

## Synonyms/Related phrase

- Portray
- Illustrate
- Represent
- Render

---

## Collocations

- depict+內容

- depict A as B: 把A刻劃/描繪成B

- A depicted in B: 在B中描繪的A

- A depicted by B: 由B所描繪的A

- accurately depict: 精準地描繪

- vividly depict: 生動地描繪

- wrongly/unfairly depict: 錯誤地/不公平地刻劃(公關或媒體報導)

---

## Examples

- The report accurately depicts our current market position.
- The CEO is depicted as a visionary leader.
- The data depicted in the chart shows significant growth.
