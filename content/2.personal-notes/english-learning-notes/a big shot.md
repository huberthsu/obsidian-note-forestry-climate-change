## Meaning

- A person of great importance or influence in a particular field or organization

---

## Word Family

- big(adj)大的、重要的
- shot(n)開槍、嘗試、人物
- 相關詞彙: big wheel(大人物), bigwig(大人物), VIP(重要人物)

## Collocations

- acts like a big shot 裝得很了不起、擺架子
- Become / Turn into a big shot 成為大人物
- Think oneself a big shot 自以為是大人物
- Mr. Big Shot

## Examples

- Don't treat me like a big shot; I'm just a regular person here.
