## Meaning

- A disadvantage, problem, or negative feature of a situation, plan, or product.

---

## Word Family

---

## Synonyms/Related phrase

---

## Collocations

- The benefits of+n+outweigh the drawbacks：某事務的優點多於缺點

- the main / major drawback: 主要的缺點

* the only drawback: 唯一的缺點
* suffer from a drawback: 飽受缺點之苦

---

## Examples

- The apartment is great, but the only drawback is the distance from the MRT station.
- Despite some disagreement, the benefits of the new policy still far outweigh the drawbacks.
