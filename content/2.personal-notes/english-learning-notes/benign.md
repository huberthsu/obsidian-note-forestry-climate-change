## Meaning

- Having no significant harm or adverse effect
- Commonly used in discussions about impacts and effects

---

## Word Family

- benignly(adv)無害地
- benignancy(n)(醫學)良性、無害
- 字根 gen（出生、種類，源自拉丁 gignere/genus）+ bene（好）— benign = bene(好) + gen(出生本性) = 天性良善的→良性的
  - malign(adj)惡意的、惡性的 — male(壞) + gen，與 benign 恰為反義字根組合
  - genuine(adj)真正的 — 同源 gen

## Collocations

- benign toward+人: 對某人的態度溫和

- benign to/for+人/事物

- benign tumor / tumour: 良性腫瘤

* benign economic environment: 良性的經濟環境
* benign market conditions: 溫和/有利的市場條件
* benign inflation: 溫和的通貨膨脹（指通膨率很低，不會傷害經濟）

---

## Examples

- The doctor said the tumor is benign, so there's no need to worry
- My grandfather is always benign toward his grandchildren.
- The climate here is benign to the growth of crops.
