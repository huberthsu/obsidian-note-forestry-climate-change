## Meaning

- (noun) A preparation applied to the face or body to improve or change the appearance, such as makeup, moisturizer, or perfume.
- (adjective) Relating to or designed to improve the appearance of someone or something, especially superficially.
- (adjective, figurative) Superficial or intended merely to improve appearance without addressing underlying issues.

---

## Word Family

- cosmetics(n)化妝品（複數、集合名詞）
- cosmetologist(n)美容師、化妝師
- cosmetology(n)美容學、化妝術
- 字根 cosm-(秩序、宇宙、裝飾，源自希臘 kosmos)— 原意「秩序」→「裝飾」→「美化外表」
  - cosmos(n)宇宙
  - cosmic(adj)宇宙的
  - cosmopolitan(adj/n)世界主義的；世界公民
  - kosmetikos(希臘) = 關於裝飾、排列的藝術

---

## Synonyms/Related phrase

- makeup: 化妝品、妝容（最常用的日常詞）
- beauty product: 美容產品（更通用、中立）
- superficial: 表面的、膚淺的（形容詞用法）
- surface-level: 表面層級的、膚淺的（形容詞）
- cosmetic change: 表面改變、無實質改善的改變

---

## Collocations

- cosmetic surgery: 整容手術、美容手術
- cosmetic products/items: 化妝品、美容產品
- cosmetic changes: 表面改變、化妝品式的改變
- purely cosmetic: 純粹表面上的
- cosmetic dentistry: 牙齒美容
- apply cosmetics: 化妝、使用化妝品

---

## Examples

- He decided to undergo cosmetic surgery to improve his self-confidence.
- Making cosmetic changes to the report won't fix the underlying data errors.
