## Meaning

- Used when examining something closely.

---

## Word Family

- investigiation(n)調查

---

## Synonyms/Related phrase

---

## Collocations

- investigate+(n)
- under investigation：正在接受調查
- conduct / carry out + an investigation into...：對……展開調查

## Examples

- The police are conducting an investigation into the fraud.
