### Mobilize $200 Billion per Year for Biodiversity From all Sources, Including $30 Billion Through International Finance

每年從所有來源籌集2000億美元用於生物多樣性保護，其中300億美元來自國際金融

Substantially and progressively increase the level of financial resources from all sources, in an effective, timely and easily accessible manner, including domestic, international, public and private resources, in accordance with Article 20 of the Convention, to implement national biodiversity strategies and action plans, by 2030 mobilizing at least 200 billion United States dollars per year, including by:

<details>
<summary>查看中文翻譯</summary>
>根據《公約》第20條，以有效、及時和易獲取的方式，大幅且逐步增加來自所有來源的財政資源，包括國內、國際、公共和私人資源，用於實施國家生物多樣性戰略和行動計劃，到2030年每年至少籌集2000億美元，具體措施包括：
</details>

- (a) Increasing total biodiversity related international financial resources from developed countries, including official development assistance, and from countries that voluntarily assume obligations of developed country Parties, to developing countries, in particular the least developed countries and small island developing States, as well as countries with economies in transition, to at least US$ 20 billion per year by 2025, and to at least US$ 30 billion per year by 2030;

<details>
<summary>查看中文翻譯</summary>
>(a) 增加已開發國家（包括官方發展援助）以及自願承擔已開發國家締約方義務的國家向發展中國家，特別是最不發達國家和小島嶼發展中國家以及經濟轉型國家提供的與生物多樣性相關的國際財政資源總額，到2025年至少達到每年200億美元，到2030年至少達到每年300億美元；
</details>

- (b) Significantly increasing domestic resource mobilization, facilitated by the preparation and implementation of national biodiversity finance plans or similar instruments according to national needs, priorities and circumstances;

<details>
<summary>查看中文翻譯</summary>
>(b) 大幅增加國內資源調動，根據國家需求、優先事項和實際情況，制定和實施國家生物多樣性融資計劃或類似工具；
</details>

- (c) Leveraging private finance, promoting blended finance, implementing strategies for raising new and additional resources, and encouraging the private sector to invest in biodiversity, including through impact funds and other instruments;

<details>
<summary>查看中文翻譯</summary>
>(c) 利用私人融資，推廣混合融資，實施籌集新增資源的策略，並鼓勵私部門投資生物多樣性，包括透過影響力投資基金和其他工具；
</details>

- (d) Stimulating innovative schemes such as payment for ecosystem services, green bonds, biodiversity offsets and credits, benefit-sharing mechanisms, with environmental and social safeguards;

<details>
<summary>查看中文翻譯</summary>
>(d) 鼓勵創新方案，例如生態系統服務付費、綠色債券、生物多樣性補償和信用、惠益分享機制，並輔以環境和社會保障措施；
</details>

- (e) Optimizing co-benefits and synergies of finance targeting the biodiversity and climate crises;

<details>
<summary>查看中文翻譯</summary>
>(e) 改善針對生物多樣性和氣候危機的融資的共同效益和協同效應；
</details>

- (f) Enhancing the role of collective actions, including by indigenous peoples and local communities, Mother Earth centric actions\[1] and non-market-based approaches including community based natural resource management and civil society cooperation and solidarity aimed at the conservation of biodiversity;

<details>
<summary>查看中文翻譯</summary>
>(f) 加強集體行動的作用，包括原住民和地方社區的行動、以地球為中心的行動[1]以及非市場化方法，包括以社區為基礎的自然資源管理以及旨在保護生物多樣性的民間社會合作與團結； 
</details>

- (g) Enhancing the effectiveness, efficiency and transparency of resource provision and use.

<details>
<summary>查看中文翻譯</summary>
>(g) 提高資源供給和利用的有效性、效率和透明度。
</details>

- Mother Earth-centric actions: Ecocentric and rights-based approach enabling the implementation of actions towards harmonic and complementary relationships between peoples and nature, promoting the continuity of all living beings and their communities and ensuring the non-commodification of environmental functions of Mother Earth.

<details>
<summary>查看中文翻譯</summary>
>以地球為中心的行動：以生態為中心、以權利為基礎的方法，促進人與自然之間和諧互補的關係，維護所有生物及其社區的延續，並確保地球的環境功能不會被商品化。
</details>

**Following are the guidance notes prepared by the Secretariat for Target 19**

##### A. Why is this target important? 為什麼這個目標很重要？

The Kunming-Montreal Global Biodiversity Framework will be implemented primarily through activities at the national and subnational levels, with supporting actions at the regional and global levels. However, the capacity for implementing the Convention in terms of human, technical and financial resources is limited in many countries, especially in developing countries. Together with other means of implementation, the mobilization of adequate financial resources is critical for achieving the goals and targets of the Framework.

<details>
<summary>查看中文翻譯</summary>
>《昆明-蒙特婁全球生物多樣性框架》將主要透過國家和地方層級的活動來實施，並輔以區域和全球層面的支援行動。然而，許多國家，特別是發展中國家，在人力、技術和財政資源方面實施公約的能力有限。除了其他實施手段外，調動充足的財政資源對於實現該框架的目標和指標至關重要。
</details>

##### B. Explanation of the target and its elements 目標及其要素的解釋

The main focus of this target is increasing the amount of financial resources for the implementation of national biodiversity strategies and actions to \$200 per year by 2030. To accomplish this, the target sets a number of distinct elements:

<details>
<summary>查看中文翻譯</summary>
>該目標的主要重點是到2030年將用於實施國家生物多樣性戰略和行動的財政資源增加到每年200美元。為達成此目標，此目標設定了若干具體要素：
</details>

- **From all sources –** The resources mobilized for implementing national biodiversity strategies and action plans should come from all sources. This includes domestic, international, public and privatesources.

<details>
<summary>查看中文翻譯</summary>
>來自所有來源－用於實施國家生物多樣性策略和行動計畫的資源應來自所有來源，包括國內、國際、公共和私人來源。
</details>

- **Timely and easily accessible manner –** Bearing in mind the time frame covered by the Kunming-Montreal Global Biodiversity Framework, the resources to support its implementation need to be made available in a time and manner that they can effectively used by Parties. This element of the target links to the final element of the target calling for the effectiveness, efficiency and transparency of resource provision and use to be enhanced;

<details>
<summary>查看中文翻譯</summary>
>- 及時且方便地獲取資源－考慮到《昆明-蒙特婁全球生物多樣性框架》的時間框架，支持其實施的資源必須以締約方能夠有效利用的方式和時間提供。此目標要素與目標的最後一個要素相聯繫，即提高資源提供和使用的有效性、效率和透明度；
</details>

- **International financial resources –** The target specifies that international financial resources provided by developed countries, and countries that voluntarily assume obligations of developed country Parties, and to developing countries, should be increased. By 2025 these funds should reach $20 billion per year, and by 2030 they should reach $30 billion per year. Official development assistance is identified in the target as one means of accomplshing this. The target further specifies the need to pay particular attention to the needs of the least developed countries and small island developing States, as well as countries with economies in transition,

<details>
<summary>查看中文翻譯</summary>
>- 國際財政資源－此目標明確指出，已開發國家、自願承擔已開發國家締約方義務的國家以及發展中國家提供的國際財政資源應增加。到2025年，這些資金應達到每年200億美元，到2030年應達到每年300億美元。官方發展援助被該目標列為實現這一目標的一種手段。該目標進一步明確指出，需要特別關注最不發達國家、小島嶼發展中國家以及經濟轉型國家的需求。
</details>

- **Domestic resource mobilization** – The target calls for domestic resource mobilization to increase signigantly and specifies that this increase can be facilitated through the preparation and implementation of national biodiversity finance plans or similar instruments

<details>
<summary>查看中文翻譯</summary>
>- 國內資源調動－該目標呼籲大幅增加國內資源調動，並明確指出，可透過制定和實施國家生物多樣性融資計劃或類似工具來促進這一增長。
</details>

- **Private sector finance** – The target identifies different mechanisms through which the private sector could contribute to the implementation of national biodiversity strategies and actions. These include blended finance, developing funding strategies, impact funds and other instruments that encourage private sector involvement.

<details>
<summary>查看中文翻譯</summary>
>- 私部門融資－此目標確定了私部門可以為國家生物多樣性策略和行動的實施做出貢獻的不同機制。這些機制包括混合融資、制定融資策略、影響力基金以及其他鼓勵私部門參與的工具。
</details>

- **Innovative finance schemes** – The target identifies innovative finance schemes, such as payment for ecosystem services, green bonds, biodiversity offsets and credits, and benefit-sharing mechanisms, as a means of mobilization resources for implementation. This element also notes that such shcemes should have environmental and social safeguards.

<details>
<summary>查看中文翻譯</summary>
>- 創新融資方案－此目標指出，生態服務給付、綠色債券、生物多樣性抵換和信用以及惠益分享機制等創新融資方案，可作為調動資源以實施相關措施的手段。該要素也指出，此類方案應具備環境和社會保障措施。'
</details>

- **Synergies with climate finance** –Biodiversity loss and climate change and linked societal challenges. The resources mobilized to address these challenges are potentially synergistic and offer opportunities for efficiencies which should be considered in the actions to reach this target.

<details>
<summary>查看中文翻譯</summary>
>- 與氣候融資的協同作用—生物多樣性喪失、氣候變遷及其相關的社會挑戰。為應對這些挑戰而調動的資源具有潛在的協同效應，並提供了提高效率的機會，在實現這一目標的行動中應予以考慮。
</details>

- **Collective action** – Collective action refers to actions taken by groups of people towards a common goal or objective. Many groups, including indigenous peoples and local communities, act for biodiversity. While these actions do not necessarily generate resources, they do have value, which should be accounted for as part of this target. This also applies to Mother Earth- centric actions and other non-market-based approaches to biodiversity.

<details>
<summary>查看中文翻譯</summary>
>- 集體行動－集體行動是指一群人為實現共同目標或目的所採取的行動。許多群體，包括原住民和當地社區，都致力於保護生物多樣性。雖然這些行動未必能創造資源，但它們確實具有價值，這應納入本目標的考量。這同樣適用於以地球為中心的行動以及其他非市場化的生物多樣性保護方法。
</details>

##### C. Links to other elements of the Kunming-Montreal Global Biodiversity Framework, and other frameworks and processes 與《昆明-蒙特婁全球生物多樣性框架》其他要素及其他框架和進程的聯繫

- Actions to reach Target 19 should take into account all of the considerations for implementation identified in [section C of the Kunming-Montreal Global Biodiversity Framework](https://www.cbd.int/gbf/introduction/).

- This target directly relates to Goal D of the Kunming-Montreal Global Biodiversity Framework. It will also indirectly support the attainment of the other goals of the framework by ensuring that the necessary resources are available for implementation. This target will also indirectly support the attainment of all targets in the framework. Conversely, progress towards this target would be supported by actions to reach targets [14](https://www.cbd.int/gbf/targets/14/), [15](https://www.cbd.int/gbf/targets/15/) and [18](https://www.cbd.int/gbf/targets/18/).

<details>
<summary>查看中文翻譯</summary>
>此目標與《昆明-蒙特婁全球生物多樣性框架》目標D直接相關。它還將透過確保提供必要的資源來間接支持框架其他目標的實現。此目標也將間接支持框架內所有目標的實現。反之，實現這一目標的進展將得益於為達成目標14、15、18
</details>

- Elements of this target were previously addressed by [Aichi Biodiversity Target 20](https://www.cbd.int/aichi-targets/target/20).
