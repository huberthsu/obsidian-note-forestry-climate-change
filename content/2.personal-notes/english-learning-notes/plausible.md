## Meaning

- Seemingly reasonable or probable; appearing worthy of belief or approval; believable or credible at first glance

---

## Word Family

- plausibility(n)可信性、似真性
- plausibly(adv)似乎合理地、可信地
- implausible(adj)似乎不合理的、難以置信的
- applaud(v)鼓掌(同源詞)
- 字根 plaudere(源自拉丁 — 意為「鼓掌」、「贊同」、「認可」)
  - plausible 原意為「值得鼓掌認可」→「似乎合理」

---

## Synonyms/Related phrase

- reasonable

## Collocations

- plausible explanation/excuse/reason/scenario/alternative: 似乎合理的解釋/藉口/原因/情景/替代方案
- entirely plausible: 完全合理的
- highly plausible: 非常可信的
- sound/seem + plausible: 聽起來/看起來合理

---

## Examples

- Although this marketing idea sounds highly plausible, we still need more data to back it up.
