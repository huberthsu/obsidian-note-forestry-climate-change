## Meaning

- Used to formally request additional time to complete something

---

## Word Family

---

## Synonyms/Related phrase

- more time

---

## Collocations

- An extension to the deadline / to the contract：對截止日期 / 合約的延長

* An extension of time / of the deadline：時間 / 截止日期的延長
* Ask for/request an extension：請求延期
* A+時間+extension：為時...的延期
  - 例如: A two-day extension：兩天的延期

---

## Examples

- I am afraid that I need to request a one-week extension to complete the report due to an unexpected emergency.
