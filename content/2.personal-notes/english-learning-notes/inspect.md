## Meaning

- Used when examining something carefully

---

## Word Family

- inspection(n)檢查、檢驗
- inspector(n)檢查員
- inspected(adj)經檢查合格的

---

## Synonyms/Related phrase

- check out

> [!note] 特殊
> 與[[scrutinize]]的比較: inspect像品管員用來確認事物是否正常；scrutinize像偵探用放大鏡小心翼翼的仔細審查

---

## Collocations

- inspect + 對象(machinery/goods/premises): 檢查機器/貨物/場所
- inspect closely/carefully/thoroughly: 仔細/徹底檢查
- conduct an inspection: 進行檢查
- undergo inspection: 接受檢查

---

## Examples

- Engineers will inspect these machines regularly to ensure safe operation.
