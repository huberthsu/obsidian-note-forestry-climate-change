## Meaning [[English puns]]

- To encourage or promate the development of something(typically regarded as good)

---

## Word Family

- fosterong(n)寄養行為；培養過程
- fosterer(n)寄養者

---

## Synonyms/Related phrase

> [!note] 「促進/推動」群組比較
> [[foster]]、[[facilitate]]、promote、stimulate、encourage 都涉及「促進」或「推動」，但角度和主動性不同。foster(園丁) 強調主動創造環境與條件、長期養護；facilitate(潤滑油/清道夫) 是協助並移除障礙；promote(宣傳大使) 是積極推廣；stimulate(針灸/強心針) 是透過刺激激發；encourage(啦啦隊) 是用言語鼓勵。詳見 [[1.English words and phrases associations#「促進/推動」群組|關聯整理]]。

---

## Collocations

- foster an environment / culture+of+n(特質:trust/respect/innovation): 營造某特質環境/文化
  - of+名詞(特質)=用來形容環境、文化本身充滿該特質

- foster an  environment / culture+for+someone/V-ing/n: 營造對誰或某動作有利的環境
  - for+名詞(目的、目標)=環境是為了促進後面的目標

- foster an  environment / culture+where+句子: 營造一個讓某人可以做某事的環境

- foster family (n): 寄養家庭

---

## Examples

- The manager aims to foster an environment where everyone can share their ideas.
- We need to foster a culture of mutual trust.
- Our company aims to foster an environment for innovation and growth.
