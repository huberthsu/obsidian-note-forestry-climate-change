## Meaning

- Used to say that something is not likely to happen; used to emphasize doubt about whether a stated condition will actually be met

---

## Word Family

---

## Synonyms/Related phrase

- big question：很大的疑問
- uncertain：不確定的

---

## Usage/Collocations

- 用在假設句中，強調實現的可能性很小
- 用於質疑他人的假設，表達對某件事的可能性感到懷疑

---

## Examples

- If we win the lottery—and that's a big if—we will buy a house.
- A: We might finish the project by tomorrow if everyone works overtime. B: That's a big if. No one wants to stay late.
