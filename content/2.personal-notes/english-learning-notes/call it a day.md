## Meaning

- To decide to stop working or doing something and go home or rest; to finish work for the day.

---

## Word Breakdown

- **call** — 決定、宣布
- **it** — 指代這一天的工作
- **a day** — 一整天

---

## Synonyms/Related phrase

- wrap it up — 收尾、結束
- clock out — 下班、打卡離開
- quit for the day: 今天停止工作
- head home: 回家

---

## Usage/Collocations

- call it a day +時間(for now/after this task): 現在/做完這個任務後先到此為止
- plan/expect/+to+call it a day+at(準時)/by(之前)/around(左右)+特定時間點
-
- be ready to call it a day: 準備收工
- decide to call it a day: 決定收工
- Let's call it a day: 我們今天就到此為止吧

---

## Examples

- I am burned out by the meetings, so I plan to call it a day at 5.
