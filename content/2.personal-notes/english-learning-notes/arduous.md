## Meaning

- Difficult and requiring a lot of effort and perseverance; describing tasks, journeys, or undertakings that demand sustained struggle.

---

## Word Family

- arduously(adv)艱難地、費力地
- arduousness(n)艱難、費力

---

## Synonyms/Related phrase

- [[strenuous]]
- [[grueling]]

> [!note] 相關詞
> 與 [[strenuous]]、[[grueling]] 以及 demanding 都涉及「艱辛、困難」，但艱辛的來源和程度有區別。詳見[[1.English words and phrases associations#「艱辛/困難」群組|詞彙關聯整理]]的四詞群組比較。

---

## Collocations

- arduous for+someone: 對某人來說很艱難

- undergo an arduous+n: 經歷艱辛的…

- arduous task/journey/process/negotiations / discussions: 艱難的任務/旅程/過程/談判/討論

- an arduous climb / route: 艱難的登頂路線

---

## Examples

- Financial restructuring is an arduous process for this new company.
