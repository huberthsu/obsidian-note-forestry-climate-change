## Meaning

- Suffering from exhaustion due to prolonged stress or overwork; emotionally, mentally, or physically exhausted.

---

## Word Family

- burnout(n) 精疲力竭、倦怠
- burn out(phrasal v) 因過度勞累而筋疲力竭

---

## Synonyms/Related phrase

- exhausted — 精疲力竭的
- [[fatigued]] — 疲勞的、疲倦的
- [[drained]] — 筋疲力盡的
- worn out — 筋疲力盡的

> [!note] 群組比較
> 詳見 [[1.English words and phrases associations#「疲憊」群組|同主題群組：疲憊]]（與 [[fatigued]]、[[drained]] 比較）

---

## Usage/Collocations

- burned out from+something/V-ing: 因(某原因)而身心俱疲
- burned out by+ something: 因(某壓力來源)而身心俱疲
- burned out on something/V-ing: 對...感到厭倦/無力
-
- get/become burned out: 變得精疲力竭
- prevent burnout: 預防過度疲勞
- lead to/cause burnout: 導致心力交瘁
- experience / suffer from burnout: 經歷／遭受心力交瘁

---

## Examples

- My colleague is burned out from dealing with difficult clients all day.
- The engineer is burned out on writing the same code every day, so he decides to change jobs.
