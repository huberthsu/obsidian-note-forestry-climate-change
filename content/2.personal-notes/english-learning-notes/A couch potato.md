## Meaning

- Someone who spends a lot of time sitting and watching TV or being inactive

---

## Word Family

- couch potato lifestyle: 沙發馬鈴薯的生活方式
- potato(n)馬鈴薯
- couch(n)沙發
- 相關詞彙: mouse potato(電腦馬鈴薯)、armchair(扶手椅)

---

## Synonyms/Related phrase

- A mouse potato（電腦馬鈴薯）：指整天坐在電腦前上網、玩遊戲的人。

---

## Collocations

- Turn into / Become a couch potato
- Sit around like a couch potato

---

## Examples

- Hey Hubert please stop being a couch potato.
