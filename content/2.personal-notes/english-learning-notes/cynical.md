## Meaning

- Believing that people are motivated purely by self-interest; distrusting or questioning people's motives; dismissive of sincere or genuine efforts.

---

## Word Family

- cynicism(n) 憤世嫉俗、懷疑主義
- cynic(n) 憤世嫉俗的人
- cynically(adv) 憤世嫉俗地、懷疑地
- 字根 cyn（狗，源自希臘文 kyon）— cynical 源自希臘犬儒學派（Cynics）；相傳該學派哲學家生活簡樸如狗、對世俗價值嗤之以鼻，後引申為「憤世嫉俗的」
  - cynic(n)憤世嫉俗的人 — 同源

---

## Synonyms/Related phrase

- skeptical — 持懷疑態度的
- distrustful — 不信任的、疑心的
- jaded — 厭倦的、麻木的
- pessimistic — 悲觀的

---

## Collocations

- cynical about+something: 對...感到懷疑/憤世嫉俗

- a cynical view/attitude: 懷疑的看法/態度

- cynical remarks/smile/laugh:  諷刺的言論/冷笑/笑容

- become/grow cynical: 變得憤世嫉俗

- cynical ploy: 不懷好意的計謀/自私的手段/虛偽的策略

---

## Examples

- Although many employees are cynical about the company's commitment, the management still strongly denies that this is a cynical ploy.
