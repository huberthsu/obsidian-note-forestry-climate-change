## Meaning

- Begrudge(v) means to resent someone because they have or possess something; to envy them for having what you don't have. It can also mean to give something unwillingly or grudgingly.

---

## Word Family

- begrudgingly(adv)不甘心地、吝嗇地

---

## Synonyms/Related phrase

- envy 嫉妒、羨慕（側重於想擁有別人有的東西）

## Collocations

- begrudge someone something: 對某人有某東西而嫉妒
  - begrudge him his success/happiness

- begrudge V-ing: 不情願做某事

- begrudge the fact that: 不滿...的事實

- begrudgingly admit/acknowledge: 不甘心地承認

- not begrudge+n/V-ing: 毫無怨言、由衷覺得值得、大方接納
  - not begrudge anyone anything: 不嫉妒任何人任何事

---

## Examples

- No one begrudged him his promotion because everyone loves him.
- He always begrudges his brother's wealth.
- I don't begrudge spending time helping you at all.
