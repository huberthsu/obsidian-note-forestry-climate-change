## Meaning

To find a middle ground between two things.

---

## Word Family

- strike(v)罷工、打擊、達成
- balance(n)平衡、餘額
- balanced(adj)平衡的、均衡的
- 相關詞彙: find a balance(找到平衡), maintain balance(維持平衡), upset the balance(打破平衡)

## Collocations

**People need to/It is important to/We should**+strike a balance+**between A and B**

---

## Examples

- It is important to strike a balance between studying and relaxing.
