## Meaning

- To represent by a drawing, painting, or other art form.

---

## Word Family

- depiction(n)描繪、描述、肖像
- depicted(adj)被描繪的
- 字根 pict（畫，源自拉丁 pingere「畫」）（Etymonline）— depict 15 世紀初出現，意思是「描繪、用顏色畫出肖像」，源自拉丁 depictus，為 depingere「描繪、畫、素描；描述、想像」的過去分詞；depingere = de「向下」+ pingere「畫」；「用文字描寫」義 15 世紀中出現
  - 🤖 **claudian:** _字根 pingere 是「畫」，de + pingere 是「畫下來」，所以 depict 是「描繪」，也延伸為用文字描寫。_
  - picture(n)圖畫 — 拉丁 pictura「繪畫」，源自 pictus，pingere「畫、繪製、刺繡」的過去分詞
  - pigment(n)顏料 — 拉丁 pigmentum「著色物、顏料」，源自 pingere「著色、畫」

## Synonyms/Related phrase

- Portray
- Illustrate
- Represent
- Render

---

## Usage/Collocations

- depict+內容

- depict A as B: 把A刻劃/描繪成B

- A depicted in B: 在B中描繪的A

- A depicted by B: 由B所描繪的A

- accurately depict: 精準地描繪

- vividly depict: 生動地描繪

- wrongly/unfairly depict: 錯誤地/不公平地刻劃(公關或媒體報導)

---

## Examples

- The report accurately depicts our current market position.
- The CEO is depicted as a visionary leader.
- The data depicted in the chart shows significant growth.
