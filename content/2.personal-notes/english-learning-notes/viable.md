## Meaning

- Capable of working successfully
- Used in discussions about sustainability and practical solutions.

---

## Word Family

- viability(n)可行性、生存能力
- viably(adv)可行地、能存活地

---

## Synonyms/Related phrase

- [[feasible]]

> [!note] 與 [[feasible]] 的比較
> viable 強調「能夠長期成功、持續運作」；feasible 強調「實踐上/技術上可以實現、可以執行」。
> viable 涵義更強（既可行 + 長期能成功），feasible 只確保「能做」，不保證成功或永續。
> example: The business model is technically **feasible**, but it's not financially **viable** without additional investment. (這個商業模式在技術上可以實現，但沒有額外投資的話經濟上無法存活。)

---

## Collocations

- It is viable to+v
- v-ing is a viable option/solution

---

## Examples

- In the long run, switching to green energy is an economically viable option.
