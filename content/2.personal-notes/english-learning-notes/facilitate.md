## Meaning

- To make something easier or possible; to help progress or enable something to happen more easily.

---

## Word Family

- facility(n) 便利、設施、能力
- facilitation(n) 便利、促進
- facilitator(n) 推動者、協調者
- facilitative(adj) 有助於的、促進的

---

## Synonyms/Related phrase

- enable: 使能夠、使可能
- promote: 促進、推動
- assist: 協助、幫忙
- expedite: 加快、促進

---

## Collocations

- facilitate + n(learning/communication/process/collaboration): 促進...

- facilitate something between someone: 促進了...之間的...

- facilitate the process: 便利程序

- help facilitate: 幫助促進

---

## Examples

- The app facilitates transactions between buyers and sellers.
- A supervisor's job is to facilitate collaboration between team members.
