## Meaning

- Someone who behaves as though they know everything and refuses to listen to others.

---

## Word Family

- know(v)知道、瞭解
- know-it-all(n)自以為是的人、萬事通
- knowall(n)同 know-it-all
- 相關詞彙: wise guy(聰明人), smarty-pants(自作聰明的人)

---

---

## Collocations

- Don't act like/Stop being + a know it all
- Sound like a know-it-all（聽起來很自以為是 / 像在說教）
- Mr. Know-it-all

---

## Examples

- Nobody wants to talk to him because he is such **a know-it-all**
- Nobody likes a know-it-all. 沒人喜歡自以為是、愛說教的人
