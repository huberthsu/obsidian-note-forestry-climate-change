## Meaning

- having or showing subtle, small, and delicate differences in meaning, feeling, or tone

---

## Word Family

- nuance(n) 細微差別、細微差異、細節
- nuanced(adj) 有細微差別的、精妙的、微妙的
- 字根 nub（雲，源自拉丁 nubes「雲、霧、蒸氣」，PIE 字根 \*sneudh-「霧」）（Etymonline）— nuance 源自法語 nuance「細微差別、色彩濃淡」，源自 nuer「加上陰影」，源自 nue「雲」，再源自拉丁 nubes；法語原本指「雲的不同色彩」；nuanced 是動詞 nuance 的過去分詞形容詞，表示「呈現細微層次的」
  - 🤖 **claudian:** _字根 nub 源自「雲」，引申為色彩和意思的細微變化。nuanced 就像雲的色調變化一樣微妙精細，表示「有細微差別、精妙深入的」。_

## Usage/Collocations

- nuanced+something: 有細微差別的...

- nuanced understanding of+主題: 對某個主題有著細膩、深刻且全面的理解

- require/need a nuanced approach: 需要精妙的方法

- a nuanced understanding / perspective / argument / view

---

## Examples

- This is a highly nuanced issue, not a black-and-white question.
- Dealing with global clients requires a nuanced approach.
- The new manager demonstrated a nuanced understanding of our team's situation.
