## Meaning

- Feeling warm, comfortable, and protected, or describing something like clothing that fits closely to the body.

---

## Word Family

- snugly(adv)緊密地、舒適地、貼身地
- snugness(n)溫暖舒適、緊密合身
- snuggle(v)使舒適、依偎

---

## Synonyms/Related phrase

| 詞 | 強調重點 | 例子 |
|----|--------|-----|
| **[[cozy]]** | 整體溫馨、舒適、愜意的環境/感受 | _"Let's get cozy by the fireplace"_ — 營造溫馨舒適的氛圍 |
| **snug** | 緊密貼身 + 溫暖舒適的物理感 | _"The gloves fit snugly"_ — 手套剛好貼身合身 |

---

## Usage/Collocations

- have/provide/ensure+a snug fit: 非常合身
- fit snugly: 非常合身
- As snug as a bug in a rug: 十分溫暖舒服、非常滿意現狀。常用來形容小嬰兒、寵物，或是寒冬中縮在被窩裡的人

---

## Examples

- The cat is curling up on the sofa; she is as snug as a bug in a rug.
- These running shoes fit snugly, and they are very comfortable to walk in.
