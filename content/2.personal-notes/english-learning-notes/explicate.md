## Meaning

- To make something clear or plain; to explain something in detail (often used in academic or literary contexts).
- To unfold the meaning of a concept, text, or theory systematically and thoroughly.

---

## Word Family

- explication(n) 詳細說明、闡述
- explicable(adj) 可解釋的、可說明的
- explicitly(adv) 明確地、直言地

---

## Synonyms/Related phrase

- [[clarify]]:  闡明、釐清
- [[elaborate]]:  詳述、展開
- explain:  解釋、說明
- interpret:  解釋、詮釋

## Collocations

- explicate + text/theory/concept/meaning:  闡述 文本/理論/概念/含意
- explicate the meaning of:  闡述...的意義
- fully explicate:  完全闡述

---

## Examples

- The professor systematically explicated the complex philosophical theory in the paper.
